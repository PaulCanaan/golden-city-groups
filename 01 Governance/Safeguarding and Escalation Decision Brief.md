# Safeguarding and Escalation Decision Brief

## Document Status

**Pastoral decision brief for review with Russ; proposals and questions only.** This page does not establish church policy, legal duties, procedures, contacts, or readiness approval. See the evidence in the [[06 Resources/Leader Development/Safeguarding and Escalation Resource Gap Audit|Safeguarding and Escalation Resource Gap Audit]].

## Why This Requires Attention

DEC-027, DEC-028, and DEC-033 provide a minimum boundary: leaders are appointed and prepared, do not promise absolute confidentiality, do not act as sole crisis responders, escalate urgent and significant concerns, confirm applicable safeguards, use role-based access, and keep sensitive details out of general systems. The repository still lacks the governing policy locations, named contacts, approved reporting/incident path, role-specific conduct and removal process, home/location standard, and participant-data lifecycle needed to implement that boundary.

City Group host/leader approval and operational training are therefore blocked. Public listing and participant placement are also blocked by wider offering and system gaps. Planted's curriculum and leader are approved, but its safety/escalation gate and operational readiness remain incomplete.

## Decisions or Confirmations Needed

### Governing policy

- Which church-wide safeguarding, child-protection, vulnerable-person, volunteer/staff conduct, emergency, incident-response, pastoral-care, screening, privacy, and records policies exist?
- Where is the controlled current version of each, who owns it, and which provisions apply to City Groups, Collectives, Bible Clubs, hosts, leaders, and applicants?
- If a needed policy does not exist, who is authorized to develop it, review it, approve it, and keep it current?

### Escalation contacts

- Who is the primary pastoral/safeguarding escalation contact?
- Who is the backup, and is a separate after-hours route required?
- Who receives participant-crisis, leader-misconduct, location-safety, and Planning Center/data concerns?
- Through what access-controlled channel should leaders receive names and contact details?

### Mandatory reporting

- What authoritative legal guidance governs the church, and where is it maintained?
- Who is authorized to interpret it for church roles and keep the guidance current?
- What is the approved internal consultation and escalation path, explicitly distinguished from any legal obligation?

### Pastoral-care boundaries

- What needs must a group leader hand to a pastor or other qualified support, and to whom?
- What, if anything, should be documented after a pastoral handoff, in which restricted system, and for how long?
- What additional prayer or ministry practices are permitted only with specific training or pastoral presence?

### Participant crises

- What approved path applies to immediate danger, self-harm concerns, threats of harm, medical emergencies, and other crises?
- What is the division between immediate emergency action, church notification, documentation, and follow-up?

### Leader misconduct

- Where may a concern or allegation be reported, including when the normal recipient is implicated?
- Who may pause or remove a leader or host, and what interim participant-care and documentation boundaries apply?
- What conduct standard must a leader or host acknowledge before appointment?

### Meeting locations

- What makes a home or other meeting place suitable and approved?
- Who verifies accessibility, emergency information, private-home visibility, hazards, capacity, and contingency arrangements?
- Who may cancel, relocate, or pause a meeting when a location becomes unsafe?

### Minors and vulnerable participants

- Are minors, childcare, or vulnerable participants part of any current offering?
- If so, which approved safeguarding, supervision, consent, check-in, communication, transportation, screening, and facility requirements apply?
- Is the draft Attendance statement about under-13 Check-Ins correct, and what approved source supports it?

### Confidentiality and data

- Which applicant, participant, attendance, interview, screening, pastoral, and incident fields may be collected, and with what notice or consent?
- Who may access each record type, where is it stored, and how are access, correction, retention, deletion, export, and data-issue response handled?
- What minimum interview outcome record is sufficient without retaining unnecessary pastoral or discernment narrative?

### Incident documentation

- Does a church incident form and restricted record system already exist?
- What events require a record, who submits and reviews it, who is notified, and what retention/correction rules apply?
- How is an incident record kept separate from general Planning Center group notes, chat, email, and this repository?

### Approval and review process

- Who approves the final implementation package and role-specific training?
- What evidence must exist before host approval, leader access, public listing, participant placement, and Planted's first meeting?
- Who reviews the policies and contacts after launch, and on what trigger or cadence?

## Recommended Minimum Before Launch

**Proposal — not approved policy.** Use the smallest package that makes the existing decisions operational:

1. Controlled links to the applicable church-wide policies and authoritative legal guidance.
2. A role-based contact card with primary, backup, urgent, misconduct/recusal, data, and after-hours routes as applicable.
3. A short approved escalation guide covering abuse/safeguarding disclosure, immediate danger/self-harm, medical emergency, leader misconduct, unsafe location, sensitive data, and care beyond role.
4. An approved incident form or link to the existing restricted system, with access and retention boundaries.
5. Approved leader/host role and conduct expectations, screening-if-required implementation, and home/location approval evidence.
6. Approved Planning Center/applicant fields, permissions, consent, access review/removal, correction, retention, and deletion settings.
7. One required training segment with a simple understanding check and completion record.
8. Offering-specific evidence and Russ's or his designated pastor's final readiness approval.

This package should be complete before host approval and operational leader training. The applicable pieces must also be complete before public listing, participant placement, or Planted's first meeting.
