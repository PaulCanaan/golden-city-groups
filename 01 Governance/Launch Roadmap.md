## Launch Readiness Gates

Keep the ministry within Golden City Church's mission, the three approved branches, and Belonging, Beholding, Becoming. Confirm who is accountable, which people are approved to lead, what preparation they completed, where and when the group meets, who can join and how, and what safeguarding and pastoral escalation procedures apply. Use the [[07 Operations/Workflows/Group Launch Readiness#Offering Readiness Checks|offering readiness checks]] for the operational evidence, including a tested participant route where applicable.
