Russ intends for them to be prayerfully identified, invited, discerned, and trained from within the Launch Team. This is the confirmed launch-season leadership pipeline, not a permanent restriction on how leaders may be developed in later seasons.

A sign-up or interview expresses interest and begins assessment; neither constitutes appointment or final group assignment.

## Confirmed Ministry Environments

Golden City Groups uses four complementary environments:

- **City Groups — Community where you live:** location-based groups in homes or local meeting places. They use the Weekend Message Group Guide to build relationships, pray, and apply the weekend message.
- **Bible Clubs — Study the Bible together:** discussion-based, facilitator-led gatherings at a designated central gathering place that work through a book or section of the Bible.
- **Classes — Learn and get equipped:** teacher-led, structured classroom environments using topic-specific curriculum.
- **Collectives — Gender-specific ministry environments:** centralized ministry gatherings currently expressed through Men's Collective and Women's Collective.

Simply put: City Groups provide community, pastoral connection, and weekend-message application; Bible Clubs study the Bible; Classes equip people around a topic and develop disciple-makers; and Collectives provide gender-specific community and formation. All four should participate in Belonging, Beholding, and Becoming according to their distinct purpose. Collectives are not a subtype of the other environments.
