# Theological Framework

## Document Status

**Confirmed framework with explicitly marked areas requiring further development.** This document guides ministry design but does not replace Golden City Church's statement of beliefs.

## Church Foundations

The ministry is:

- Centered on Jesus
- Grounded in Scripture as the church's authority
- Dependent on the power and work of the Holy Spirit
- Located within the life and mission of the church
- Directed toward faithful discipleship, service, flourishing, and gospel witness

The church's official public theological statements are maintained at [What We Believe](https://www.goldencity.church/beliefs). New doctrinal claims require pastoral review rather than inference from Paul's interests or ministry materials.

## Formation Over Information Alone

Christian knowledge matters, but the goal is not the accumulation of information. Biblical and theological learning should contribute to worship, prayer, repentance, freedom in Christ, obedience, community, service, wisdom, mission, and Christlike maturity.

Formation is embodied and ecclesial. It happens through the work of the Spirit as people attend to Christ in Scripture and participate in the shared life of the church.

## Belonging, Beholding, Becoming

The central ministry framework is:

**Belonging → Beholding → Becoming**

The arrow describes a formative relationship, not a rigid sequence. The three dimensions continually reinforce one another.

### Belonging

People are welcomed into relationship with God and his people. Belonging includes:

- Hospitality
- Christ-centered fellowship
- Covenant community
- Mutual care and encouragement
- Participation
- Friendship
- Generosity
- Embodied life together

Belonging is more than social comfort. It is participation in the life of Christ's people and should create room for newcomers, mature believers, care, service, worship, and mission.

### Beholding

People encounter, attend to, worship, and learn from Christ through:

- Scripture
- Prayer
- Worship
- The Holy Spirit
- Biblical and theological teaching
- The life and witness of the church

Beholding keeps Jesus as the ministry's pursuit, Scripture as its authority, and the Holy Spirit as its power. It should lead to faith, repentance, freedom, love, obedience, and worship rather than information alone.

### Becoming

Through belonging and beholding, people are formed into the likeness of Christ and equipped to participate in God's mission. Becoming includes:

- Faithful everyday discipleship
- Freedom and obedience in Christ
- Service to the church and neighbor
- Generosity
- Growth in wisdom and character
- Participation in the witness of the gospel
- Leadership and apprenticeship where appropriate
- Helping form others

Becoming is not synonymous with receiving a ministry title. Mature disciples may form others through households, friendships, vocation, hospitality, service, teaching, leadership, and witness.

## Integrated Formation

The dimensions operate together:

- Belonging creates relational contexts in which people behold Christ.
- Beholding reshapes how people belong to and care for one another.
- Belonging and beholding contribute to becoming like Christ.
- Becoming leads people to welcome, teach, serve, give, witness, and form others.
- Those practices deepen belonging and direct attention back to Christ.

Ministry content should not treat one dimension as sufficient by itself.

## Relationship to Golden City Church's Mission

- **Forge faith:** supported through Scripture, prayer, worship, community, practice, and theological formation.
- **Find freedom in Christ:** supported through the gospel, repentance, pastoral care, prayer, life in the Spirit, and faithful community.
- **Follow Jesus fully:** supported through obedience, shared life, service, generosity, mission, and helping form others.
- **Flourishing:** understood as life ordered toward Christ, the good of his church, love of neighbor, and gospel witness—not merely personal comfort or success.

## Theological Guardrails

- Do not reduce belonging to social affinity.
- Do not reduce beholding to classroom learning.
- Do not reduce becoming to volunteering, leadership titles, or numerical growth.
- Do not separate the Spirit's work from scriptural authority.
- Do not present Paul's theological interests as official church positions without approval.
- Do not make Groups and Classes substitutes for gathered worship or the broader life of the church.

## Approved Pastoral Baseline

Under DEC-033, Groups and Classes:

- teach freedom in Christ through the gospel, repentance, grace, truth, prayer, the Spirit's work, and faithful community without promising a particular emotional or circumstantial outcome;
- describe flourishing as life ordered toward Christ, love of neighbor, holiness, service, and gospel witness rather than comfort or success;
- expect Scripture-shaped prayer and dependence on the Holy Spirit in every environment;
- keep detailed spiritual-gifts, discernment, deliverance, and prayer-ministry practices within boundaries explicitly approved by Russ and supported by training and pastoral presence; and
- escalate significant pastoral or spiritual-care concerns rather than making a group leader the sole responder.

DEC-034 later changed the operating architecture, not this pastoral baseline. The baseline applies across City Groups, Collectives, and Bible Clubs and to any future approved Class.

## Open Theological Questions

- Will Belonging, Beholding, Becoming be public-facing church language or primarily an internal ministry framework?
- How should baptism, communion, membership, and church discipline relate to the participant journey?
