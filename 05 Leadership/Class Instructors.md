## Instructor Purpose

Class instructors serve the ministry's concentrated intellectual and spiritual formation environment. They should cultivate both:

- intellectual formation through faithful handling of Scripture, theology, interpretation, and clear teaching;
- spiritual formation through prayer, worship, reflection, obedience, service, and embodied response.

An instructor is not merely a lecturer. The role requires pastoral awareness, humility, theological accountability, and the ability to connect understanding with faithful life.
