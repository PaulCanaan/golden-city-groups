## What It Is

Group Leader Collaboration is a gathering of current group leaders, and it is all about supporting them. Leaders:

- **share** their experiences and struggles;
- **learn** from one another;
- **are poured into and equipped** for the work they carry; and
- **align** with the mission, vision, and values of Golden City Groups.

Content may include the Belonging, Beholding, Becoming formation framework, the [[06 Resources/Leader Development/CoJourner|CoJourner]] Program, or other workshops. DEC-082 also authorizes the framework in the two leader guides planned for the public resources page.

## Why It Exists

Group leaders serve together for the body of the church, but they do not literally serve together. A City Group leader leads in their own living room on their own night; a Collective co-lead and a Bible Club facilitator do parallel work in different rooms, often at the same hour. They belong to one ministry team that almost never sees one another work. Isolation is the role's default condition, not a failure of character, and connection among leaders has to be built deliberately because it will not happen on its own. Only the ministry can put leaders in the same room. The full argument is in [[Supporting Leaders and Groups]].

## How It Differs from Training and Check-Ins

A collaboration is a space where group leaders share experience and learn from one another: not training, and not a check-in with the Groups Director. Those three things do different work, and the difference is the point:

- **Training** teaches a leader what the ministry has already decided.
- **A check-in** lets the Groups Director care for a leader and notice needs ([[07 Operations/Workflows/Leader Support Check-In|Leader Support Check-In]]).
- **A collaboration** lets leaders learn from each other's actual weeks: the thing that worked in Thornton that nobody in Broomfield would have thought of.

Only the third one produces knowledge the ministry did not already have. It is also the only one where a leader discovers that their hardest month is not unique to them.

CoJourner may run during Collaborations, so that training happens alongside peers rather than in a separate room (DEC-045, DEC-056). Paul is also available to leaders pastorally, through a sign-up calendar for one-on-one support (DEC-064).

## Who Comes

All current group leaders. The leaders' Church Center group chat (DEC-057) carries the invitation and details; its members are City Group hosts, Collective leaders and co-leaders, table leaders and discussion facilitators, and class instructors.

## Boundaries

- **Not an escalation path.** Significant pastoral, safeguarding, or crisis concerns follow the church's care and escalation path (DEC-028, DEC-033), not a group conversation.
- **What leaders share stays with them.** Struggles, stories, and names shared at a Collaboration do not go into this repository. Gathering records hold only non-sensitive facts: that it happened, a rough count, the topics covered, and what comes next.
- **Homes.** A Collaboration hosted in a home keeps its address private, and DEC-028's home-gathering safeguards apply.
- **Attendance is not appointment.** Being at a Collaboration doesn't appoint, approve, or commission anyone (DEC-027).
