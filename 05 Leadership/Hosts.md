# Hosts

## Document Status

Fall 2026 host recruitment began with the Launch Team (DEC-040). Future openings can receive interest from the congregation ([[08 Archive/Decisions/Decision History#DEC-091 — Congregational Leader Interest after the First Launch|DEC-091]]). Confirm the host role and home readiness under DEC-027 and the agreement.

## Pending Decisions

- Host qualifications and approval authority
- Expectations for hospitality, facility readiness, and group access
- Safeguarding requirements for homes, minors, childcare, and vulnerable people
- Relationship between host and group leader responsibilities
- Training and escalation expectations

The DEC-027 minimum standard applies. No home or public meeting location should be represented as approved because a potential host expresses interest.
