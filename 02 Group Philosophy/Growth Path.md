# Growth Path

## Document Status

**Working orientation page; mixed authority.** This page shows how a person actually moves through Golden City Church's Groups and Classes Ministry — into a branch, through it, and toward responsibility — and how the branches themselves are permitted to develop. It condenses governing material rather than adding to it.

## Purpose

Help leaders offer a truthful next step without treating people as entries in an automatic ministry pipeline. A person should always be able to hear what is actually available to them right now, and never be told that something has been decided about them that has not.

## Entry Points

People arrive through relationship and through systems, and the two rarely arrive in the same order.

| Entry | What it is | What happens next |
|---|---|---|
| A Sunday gathering or a friendship | The ordinary way most people come | A personal invitation and one clear next step |
| A Connection Card expressing interest in Groups | Participation interest | The Groups Director sends the [[07 Operations/Planning & Communication/Participant Welcome and Groups Introduction\|welcome and Groups introduction]] |
| A Church Center request to join an offering | A request, not a placement | The offering's owner confirms, places, waitlists, redirects, or declines pastorally |
| `Lead a Group` or the group-leading option on the `Serve Team Interest Form` | Leader interest | Enters the Group Leader workflow at Step 0 |

Participation interest and leader interest are different things, and a Connection Card does not become a leadership conversation. The Connection Card's fields, owner, consent language, and routing are not yet documented; see the September 22, 2026 [[00 Dashboard/Inbox|Inbox]] item.

## The Three Branches as Next Steps

The three branches are one ministry serving [[Belonging, Beholding, Becoming]] according to distinct purposes. When helping someone find a next step, name the purpose, not the program.

| Branch | Purpose | Serves the person who needs | Current form |
|---|---|---|---|
| **City Groups** | Life together | Community, pastoral connection, prayer, and everyday discipleship close to home | Location-based groups |
| **Collectives** | Shared encouragement | Gender-specific community and formation among men or among women | Men's Collective and Women's Collective |
| **Bible Clubs** | Rooted in Scripture | Sustained, discussion-based attention to a book or section of Scripture | Facilitator-led study; *Planted* is the first confirmed offering |

**No branch is a prerequisite for another.** A person may begin wherever their current need is best served, participate in more than one, or move between them as their season changes. Movement is relational and prayerful, not a sequence to be completed.

**Classes are not a fourth branch.** They are a future development path under Bible Clubs. Do not offer one as a current next step.

## Participant Path

**Proposed, not an approved program sequence.** Movement is relational, prayerful, and non-linear.

1. A person encounters Golden City Church through a gathering or a relationship.
2. The person receives a clear invitation and one simple next step.
3. A ministry leader follows up personally and helps connect the person to an appropriate, **launch-ready** environment.
4. A City Group, Collective, or Bible Club serves the person's current need according to its distinct purpose.
5. The person grows through belonging, beholding Christ, and faithful becoming — in freedom, service, generosity, and gospel witness.
6. Gifts, capacity, and service opportunities are discerned relationally, through participation rather than through application.
7. Some people enter approved preparation for bounded ministry responsibility.
8. Mature disciples help form others, with or without holding a formal title.

Steps 6 through 8 are where this path meets the leader pipeline below. The transition is a conversation, not a promotion.

## From Participation to Responsibility

**The stages are confirmed under DEC-042. What happens inside them is not.** [[07 Operations/Planning Center Groups#3. Move prospective leaders from intake to launch|Planning Center Groups]] governs the workflow logic, ownership, and advancement; [[07 Operations/Planning & Communication/Communication|Communication]] holds the message at each step.

**Interest → Welcome / Contact → Group Leader Conversation → Training → Launch**

- **Interest** is Step 0, an intake trigger rather than a stage. It begins discernment and confers nothing.
- **Welcome / Contact** is the Groups Director's personal follow-up and the invitation to talk.
- **Group Leader Conversation** is the relational and discerning meeting — story, experience, and the right next step discerned together. Planning Center records it internally as *Interviewed*; never use that word with the person.
- **Training** prepares a person for the role. Attending it establishes neither appointment nor assignment.
- **Launch** is reached only after the group has actually begun meeting.

Three boundaries govern the whole sequence:

- **Interest is not appointment.** Under DEC-027, appointment requires a pastoral or delegated relational conversation, alignment, a role description with escalation boundaries, appropriate preparation, agreement to confidentiality and safeguarding practices, capacity for the rhythm, any church-required screening, and an explicitly recorded appointment. Russ or his designated pastor appoints; Paul coordinates within DEC-021.
- **For Fall 2026, initial City Group leadership stays inside the Launch Team** under DEC-018. Public communication must not imply that anyone may immediately become a City Group leader.
- **CoJourner is the selected training architecture** — four biweekly sessions over roughly two months, intended for completion before the Spring 2027 launch (DEC-045). It does not replace role-specific preparation, safeguarding, assessment, appointment, or readiness approval.

## How the Branches Themselves Develop

**Confirmed long-term direction under DEC-035. This permits development; it approves no offering.** Each future expression still needs an owner, preparation, a participant path, and readiness approval before it exists.

| Branch | Approved development pathway |
|---|---|
| **City Groups** | Location-based groups → future home groups → activity-based groups → demographic-based groups → other approved expressions |
| **Collectives** | Men's and Women's Collectives → broader Men's Ministry and Women's Ministry |
| **Bible Clubs** | Facilitator-led study → teacher-led Classes on a topic or area of growth |

Run Club, Youth City Group, and Young Adults Group remain deferred under DEC-022. Financial and marriage classes are examples of the *type* of future Class, not approved offerings. Freedom Groups remain a future-ministry concept only.

A branch develops when real pastoral need and real leader capacity call for it — not because a page, a folder, or a good idea exists.

## Operating Boundaries

- Connect people only to environments that are actually launch-ready.
- Use a tested participant path and give a timely human response.
- Do not promise placement, leadership, appointment, or readiness before review.
- Say plainly which of the four statuses applies: expressing interest, requesting to join, registering, or joining.
- Keep sensitive pastoral, safeguarding, screening, and participant information out of this repository.
- Follow DEC-032 for participant-system ownership and PRO-030 for the remaining participant-path and data decisions.
