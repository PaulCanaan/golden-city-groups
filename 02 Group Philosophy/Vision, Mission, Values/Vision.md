# Vision

## Confirmed church vision

> To see our city fully following Jesus.

This is Golden City Church's vision. Groups Ministry does not create a separate vision; it serves this one.

The church's public language emphasizes Christ-centered fellowship, community, wholehearted worship, purposeful prayer, transformed lives, everyday commitment to Christ, and hope for the city. These convictions give the ministry its horizon: people rooted in Christ, formed within his church, and living as faithful witnesses among their neighbors.

## What future are we prayerfully seeking?

To follow Jesus fully is to bring the whole of life under his gracious lordship. It includes trusting him, learning from him, receiving freedom in him, obeying his teaching, sharing life with his people, and participating in his mission. It does not mean claiming sinless maturity or measuring discipleship by visible activity alone. It names a life continually being formed by Christ through Scripture, the Holy Spirit, and the life of the church (Matthew 28:18–20).

The vision joins the church's internal formation to its outward witness. People are gathered and formed in Christ so that they may love God, love their neighbors, serve faithfully, practice hospitality, and bear witness to the gospel. Community is therefore not an inward destination. It is a Christ-centered shared life that makes room for others and seeks their good.

The city is not an abstract ministry territory. It is made up of actual neighbors, households, schools, workplaces, and communities. Seeking to see the city follow Jesus means loving the people and places God has placed near us, not simply extending the reach of an organization. The ministry should value faithful presence and trustworthy relationships before the appearance of expansion (Matthew 22:37–40).

## How Groups Ministry serves the vision

**Groups** serve the vision by giving people a primary environment for Christ-centered shared life, pastoral care, prayer, encouragement, hospitality, and everyday discipleship. They help following Jesus become relational and embodied.

**Future Classes under Bible Clubs** may serve the vision through deeper biblical, theological, and ministry formation when specifically approved.

Together, the three branches connect belonging, deeper formation, service, and gospel witness. They are servants of the vision, not measures of its success.

## Ministry interpretation and boundaries

The vision itself is **confirmed church language**. The ministry interpretation above explains how Groups Ministry participates in it. Specific expressions, future Class offerings, city strategies, ministry practices, and measures of flourishing remain unconfirmed unless recorded in `00 Dashboard/Decision Log.md`.

The ministry should not claim that it can produce spiritual transformation through programming. It can cultivate faithful environments, equip leaders, love people, teach Scripture, pray, serve, and depend on God for fruit.
