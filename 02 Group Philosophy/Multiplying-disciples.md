# Multiplying Disciples

## Document Status

**Draft long-term direction; not a launch program.** Detailed multiplication systems are intentionally deferred. This page states the minimum formation posture supported by current documents.

## Purpose

Mature disciples increasingly welcome, serve, teach, encourage, witness, and help form others. Multiplication begins with faithful participation and Christlike service, not with creating titles, groups, or layers before leaders and pastoral capacity exist.

## Simple Development Pattern

1. Participate faithfully in the life of the church.
2. Practice belonging, beholding, and becoming.
3. Serve others through appropriate, bounded opportunities.
4. Receive relational and pastoral discernment.
5. Complete role-specific preparation when invited.
6. Accept responsibility only through explicit appointment.
7. Help others grow while remaining supported and accountable.

## Current Boundary

For the fall 2026 launch, City Group leaders come from the Launch Team through the confirmed pathway. A broader apprenticeship model, formal coaching layer, multiplication targets, and process for starting new groups remain future proposals requiring pastoral direction and real ministry capacity.
