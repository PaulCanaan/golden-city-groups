# Beholding

## Document Status

**Draft synthesis of the confirmed theological framework.** This page explains an approved formation dimension; it does not add doctrinal positions or prescribe unapproved prayer or spiritual-gifts practices.

## Purpose

Beholding directs people toward Jesus through Scripture, prayer, worship, the work of the Holy Spirit, and the life and witness of the church. It seeks attention that leads to faith, repentance, freedom, love, obedience, and worship rather than information alone.

Under DEC-049, this is how beholding is introduced publicly:

> Beholding the glory of the Son of God is the posture, orientation of life, and act of worship we take as the groups ministry.

Three words in that sentence carry weight. Beholding is a **posture** — something held, not achieved. It is an **orientation of life**, not an activity scheduled into part of a week. And it is an **act of worship**, which means it is directed at a person rather than at content about him.

## What Beholding Includes

- attending carefully to Christ in Scripture
- praying with dependence on the Holy Spirit
- receiving biblical and theological teaching within the church
- responding through worship, repentance, gratitude, and obedience
- learning to recognize truth without separating the Spirit's work from scriptural authority

## Across the Three Branches

- **City Groups:** attend to Christ through shared life, prayer, Scripture, and faithful response; Weekly Recap is optional.
- **Collectives:** attend to Christ through Scripture, prayer, and formation in gender-specific community.
- **Bible Clubs:** study and discuss a book or section of Scripture together; future Classes may provide topic-based equipping.

## Why Beholding Is the Hinge

Beholding is not a stage between welcome and formation. It is the means by which formation happens. Scripture draws the pattern directly: those who behold the glory of the Lord are transformed into the same image, from one degree of glory to another, by the Spirit (2 Corinthians 3:18).

This is why the ministry prefers formation over information alone. A group can study accurately and discuss well and still leave people unchanged, if attention never lands on Christ himself. The aim of a passage, a prayer, or a discussion is not that people understand it but that they see him.

## Guardrails

- Do not reduce beholding to content consumption or emotional experience.
- Do not present Paul's theological interests as official church positions.
- Keep detailed spiritual-gifts and prayer-ministry practices within the baseline approved by DEC-033 and any later explicitly approved training and pastoral direction.
