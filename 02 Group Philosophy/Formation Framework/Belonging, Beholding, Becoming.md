# Formation Framework: Belonging → Beholding → Becoming

## Document Status

**Confirmed formation framework (DEC-002); public-facing language confirmed under DEC-049.** The [[01 Governance/Theological Framework#Belonging, Beholding, Becoming|Theological Framework]] governs; this page is the working overview for leaders.

## The Framework

| | People experience | People encounter | People become |
|---|---|---|---|
| **[[Belonging]]** | hospitality · friendship · care · safety | | |
| **[[Beholding]]** | | Scripture · prayer · Christ · Spirit · worship | |
| **[[Becoming]]** | | | disciples · servants · leaders · disciple-makers |

The arrow describes a formative relationship, not a rigid sequence. The three dimensions continually reinforce one another:

- Belonging creates the relational context in which people behold Christ.
- Beholding reshapes how people belong to and care for one another.
- Belonging and beholding together form people into the likeness of Christ.
- Becoming leads people to welcome, teach, serve, give, and witness — which deepens belonging and directs attention back to Christ.

No dimension is sufficient by itself, and no one completes one before beginning another.

## Introducing the Framework Publicly

Under DEC-049, this framework is public-facing language, not only an internal model. Whenever the vision, mission, and values of Golden City Groups are shared, the formation model is introduced alongside them. The approved simple introduction is:

> **Belonging** — before you believe anything we believe as a church, you will find belonging when you come to a group.
>
> **Beholding** — beholding the glory of the Son of God is the posture, orientation of life, and act of worship we take as the groups ministry.
>
> **Becoming** — formation is the goal. We are becoming more like Jesus as the Holy Spirit is renewing us day by day.

Leaders may use their own words, but should not change what each dimension means. The fuller explanation for leaders is on the [[Belonging]], [[Beholding]], and [[Becoming]] pages.

## The Scriptural Pattern

The framework's internal logic follows a pattern Scripture itself draws: those who behold the glory of the Lord are transformed into the same image, from one degree of glory to another, by the Spirit (2 Corinthians 3:18) — and that renewal happens day by day (2 Corinthians 4:16). Beholding is not a stage people pass through on the way to becoming; it is the means by which becoming happens at all.

This is why the ministry prefers formation over information alone. Attention to Christ is not preparation for transformation. It is where transformation comes from.

## Across the Three Branches

All three branches serve all three dimensions according to their distinct purpose:

| Branch | Belonging | Beholding | Becoming |
|---|---|---|---|
| **City Groups — life together** | shared life, hospitality, and care close to home | Scripture, prayer, and faithful response; Weekly Recap optional | everyday discipleship, service, and witness |
| **Collectives — shared encouragement** | community among men and among women | Scripture, prayer, and formation in gender-specific community | faithful response and service |
| **Bible Clubs — rooted in Scripture** | hospitable study where questions are welcome | sustained attention to a book or section of Scripture | responding faithfully rather than stopping at interpretation |

No branch may claim one dimension as its whole assignment or treat another as someone else's work.

## Guardrails

- Do not reduce belonging to social affinity, beholding to classroom learning, or becoming to volunteering, titles, or numerical growth.
- Do not treat the framework as a program sequence, a membership pipeline, or a measure of an individual's spiritual maturity.
- Do not separate the Spirit's work from scriptural authority.
- DEC-049 approves ministry language, not new doctrine; the Theological Framework and its guardrails are unchanged.
