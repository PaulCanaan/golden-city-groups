# Becoming

## Document Status

**Draft synthesis of the confirmed theological framework.** This page explains an approved formation dimension. Specific leadership pathways, appointments, and public measures remain subject to active decisions.

## Purpose

Becoming describes formation into the likeness of Christ and faithful participation in his mission. It includes everyday obedience, freedom in Christ, service, generosity, wisdom, character, gospel witness, and helping form others.

Becoming is not synonymous with receiving a title. People may help form others through households, friendships, vocation, hospitality, service, teaching, leadership, and witness.

## What Becoming Looks Like

- practicing repentance, obedience, reconciliation, and generosity
- serving the church and neighbor with growing faithfulness
- carrying Scripture and prayer into everyday life
- using gifts within healthy authority and accountability
- helping others follow Jesus without seeking status
- accepting bounded responsibility only after appropriate discernment and preparation

## Across the Four Environments

- **City Groups:** practice the weekend message through care, prayer, service, and shared life.
- **Bible Clubs:** respond faithfully to Scripture rather than stopping at interpretation.
- **Classes:** develop understanding and practices that equip people to serve and form others.
- **Collectives:** encourage faithful response, service, and formation among men and among women.

## Guardrails

- Do not reduce maturity to attendance, volunteering, leadership titles, or numerical growth.
- Do not promise apprenticeship, appointment, or a ministry role outside the approved process.
- Treat metrics as prompts for pastoral conversation, not proof of spiritual maturity.
