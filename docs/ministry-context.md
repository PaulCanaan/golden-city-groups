# Ministry Context

## Document Status

**Confirmed governing context.** This document records approved background and church alignment. It does not replace Golden City Church's official public statements or pastoral authority.

## Project

This repository supports the Groups and Classes Ministry of Golden City Church, a church plant preparing to launch publicly on September 20, 2026.

Paul is helping develop this ministry with significant creative freedom from lead pastor Russ. Paul functions in this area as a ministry director or groups pastor. The precise title, reporting relationship, and delegated authority remain matters for pastoral clarification.

## Church Alignment

The Groups and Classes Ministry must remain aligned with Golden City Church's official mission, vision, theology, language, leadership, and overall growth orientation. It must not develop an independent identity that competes with the identity of the church.

Golden City Church's public language currently includes:

- **Vision:** To see the city fully following Jesus.
- **Mission:** To equip people to forge their faith, find freedom in Christ, and follow him fully.
- **Values:** Jesus as the church's pursuit; Scripture as its authority; the Holy Spirit as its power; people as its heart; service as its privilege; generosity as its lifestyle; and flourishing as its goal.

Authoritative public sources:

- [Golden City Church](https://www.goldencity.church/)
- [What We Believe](https://www.goldencity.church/beliefs)
- [Leadership](https://www.goldencity.church/leadership)

If the public website and a later direct pastoral decision differ, record the updated decision in `decision-log.md` rather than silently changing the ministry's alignment.

## Approved Ministry Purpose

> Groups provide the primary environment for Christ-centered community, pastoral care, prayer, mutual encouragement, and everyday discipleship. Classes provide intentional environments for deeper biblical, theological, and ministry formation, equipping people to live faithfully, serve the church, and help form others. Together they support Golden City Church in seeing people forge their faith, find freedom in Christ, and follow Jesus fully for the flourishing of the church and the witness of the gospel in the city.

This is the approved purpose of the Groups and Classes Ministry. It is a ministry-level expression of Golden City Church's identity, not a replacement for the church's mission or vision.

## Planning Posture

The ministry should support the church's launch without building structures too complicated to operate. Design work must distinguish:

- The **minimum viable launch model** needed to serve people faithfully in 2026
- The **ideal future model** that may develop as leaders, participants, and capacity grow
