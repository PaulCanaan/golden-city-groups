# Decision Log

## Purpose

The Decision Log is a governance tool, not a meeting transcript. It helps future leaders understand:

1. What has been decided?
2. What evidence supports that decision?
3. What ministry work is affected?
4. What still requires a decision?

Only record decisions that materially change the ministry's design, leadership, operations, curriculum, or governing documentation. Routine tasks, meeting notes, implementation progress, and minor content edits belong in their respective ministry or roadmap documents.

Optimize for long-term maintainability over completeness. Most entries should use the four-field default. Add context, rationale, or boundaries only when a foundational decision would otherwise be misunderstood.

## Statuses

### Decisions

- **Confirmed** — approved and governing
- **Proposed** — under discussion and not yet governing
- **Superseded** — replaced by a later decision

### Open Questions

- **Open** — a material decision is still required
- **Awaiting Pastoral Approval** — the matter cannot become governing without pastoral direction

### DEC-002 — Central Formation Framework

- **Decision:** Belonging, Beholding, Becoming is the central Groups and Classes formation framework. The dimensions are mutually reinforcing rather than isolated program stages.
- **Status:** Confirmed
- **Evidence:** Foundational ministry context approved for this repository.
- **Implications:** The framework governs Groups, Classes, Leadership, Resources, participant pathways, and evaluation. Its public use remains unresolved in OQ-002.

- **Boundary:** It governs ministry design but does not establish a rigid participant pipeline.

### DEC-003 — Groups and Classes as One Ministry

- **Decision:** Groups and Classes are complementary environments within one ministry and should not operate as disconnected departments.
- **Status:** Confirmed
- **Evidence:** Foundational ministry context approved for this repository.
- **Implications:** Groups and Classes share church alignment, formation principles, participant pathways, and leadership development while retaining distinct purposes.
- **Context:** The repository contains separate Group and Class structures, but both serve one church mission and formation system.
- **Rationale:** Groups provide the primary relational and pastoral environment; Classes provide intentional biblical, theological, and ministry formation. Each strengthens the other.
- **Boundary:** Integration does not make one environment a prerequisite for the other or erase their different contributions.

## Entry Templates

### Level 1 — Default Decision

```markdown
### DEC-XXX — Title

- **Decision:** What was decided?
- **Status:** Confirmed | Proposed | Superseded
- **Evidence:** What supports the decision?
- **Implications:** What ministry work is affected?
```

### Level 2 — Foundational Decision

Add only the fields needed to prevent future misunderstanding:

```markdown
- **Context:** What situation made the decision necessary?
- **Rationale:** Why was this direction chosen?
- **Boundary:** What does the decision not establish or authorize?
```

Optional metadata such as a date, related files, or a superseded decision may be added when materially useful.

### Open Question

```markdown
### OQ-XXX — Title

- **Status:** Open | Awaiting Pastoral Approval
- **Question:** What still requires a decision?
```
