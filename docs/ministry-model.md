# Ministry Model

## Document Status

The ministry purpose and the distinction between Groups and Classes are **confirmed**. Specific launch expressions, schedules, locations, staffing, and pathways remain unconfirmed unless recorded in `decision-log.md`.

## Approved Ministry Purpose

> Groups provide the primary environment for Christ-centered community, pastoral care, prayer, mutual encouragement, and everyday discipleship. Classes provide intentional environments for deeper biblical, theological, and ministry formation, equipping people to live faithfully, serve the church, and help form others. Together they support Golden City Church in seeing people forge their faith, find freedom in Christ, and follow Jesus fully for the flourishing of the church and the witness of the gospel in the city.

## One Ministry, Two Complementary Environments

Groups and Classes are not disconnected departments. They share:

- Golden City Church's identity and pastoral oversight
- The Belonging, Beholding, Becoming framework
- A commitment to Jesus, Scripture, the Holy Spirit, people, service, generosity, and flourishing
- A participant pathway toward faithful life in Christ
- A leadership-development ecosystem

### Groups

Groups provide the primary relational and pastoral environment. Their central contributions are:

- Christ-centered community
- Hospitality and belonging
- Pastoral awareness and mutual care
- Prayer and encouragement
- Scripture-centered shared life
- Everyday discipleship
- Service and local gospel witness
- Contexts in which gifts and emerging leaders can be recognized

Existing repository scaffolding includes men's gatherings, women's gatherings, smaller breakout groups, and geographic city groups. These are ministry-design possibilities or historical scaffolds until their launch status is confirmed.

### Classes

Classes provide intentional environments for:

- Deeper biblical and theological formation
- Biblical interpretation
- Spiritual and ministry practices
- Leadership development
- Preparation to serve the church
- Equipping people to help form others

Existing curriculum scaffolding includes Planted Bible Study, God With Us, and Covenant, Kingdom, People of God. Their presence does not confirm that they are complete or approved for launch.

Future curriculum may address covenant, temple, ecclesiology, Christian metaphysics and metanarrative, discipleship, spiritual formation, and biblical theology. These subjects are **future possibilities**, not an approved launch catalogue.

## How Groups and Classes Reinforce One Another

- Groups surface questions, needs, gifts, and emerging leaders.
- Classes provide focused learning and practice that can strengthen group life.
- Groups give participants a place to embody and apply what classes teach.
- Classes help leaders and participants handle Scripture and theology more faithfully.
- Both environments lead toward service, mission, and the formation of others.

Neither environment should become a bureaucratic prerequisite for the other.

## Participant Journey

The following is a **proposed operating pathway**, not yet an approved program sequence:

1. A person encounters Golden City Church through a Sunday gathering or relationship.
2. The person receives a clear invitation and simple next step.
3. A ministry leader follows up and helps connect the person appropriately.
4. A group provides an ongoing environment for belonging, prayer, care, Scripture, and everyday discipleship.
5. Classes become available for focused biblical, theological, or ministry formation.
6. The person grows in faithful participation, freedom in Christ, service, generosity, and gospel witness.
7. Gifts and capacity may be discerned through relationship and participation.
8. Some participants enter apprenticeship or defined ministry responsibility.
9. Mature disciples help form others with or without holding a formal leadership title.

Movement through this journey is relational and formative, not automatic or strictly linear.

## Leader Development Pathway

The following is **proposed** and requires pastoral approval:

1. Participant
2. Engaged contributor
3. Relational and pastoral discernment
4. Apprentice
5. Bounded, supervised responsibility
6. Approved leader, host, or instructor
7. Experienced leader who helps develop others
8. Coach or trainer when ministry scale and gifting warrant it

Development should consider character, theological alignment, relational trust, faithfulness, capacity, teachability, safe pastoral judgment, demonstrated skill, and explicit approval.

## Existing and Possible Group Expressions

### Existing Repository Scaffolding

- On-site men's gatherings with smaller breakout groups
- On-site women's gatherings with smaller breakout groups
- Off-site geographic city groups
- Communities named for Arvada, Broomfield, Erie, Lafayette, Louisville, Northglenn, Superior, Thornton, and Westminster

### Unconfirmed Possibilities

- Family-oriented home groups
- Additional geographic communities
- Other expressions that emerge from real pastoral need and leader capacity

No group should be represented as active solely because a repository page exists.

## Ministry Boundaries

- Groups and Classes support but do not replace Sunday worship or pastoral leadership.
- Classes must not become detached academic programs.
- Groups must not become inward-looking social circles.
- Leadership development must not become automatic promotion.
- The ideal future model must not determine launch scope apart from actual capacity.
- Confidential pastoral information must be kept out of this general repository.
