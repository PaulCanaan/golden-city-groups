# Leader Training

## Document Status

**Planned launch requirement; curriculum and schedule pending.** Initial City Group leader candidates will come from the Launch Team and enter training only after Team Night interest and relational discernment. Sign-up alone does not authorize participation as an approved leader.

## Initial Audience

- Launch Team members prayerfully identified and invited as potential City Group leaders
- Hosts only if a host pathway and requirements are approved
- Coaches only if a formal coaching layer is approved

Public leadership recruitment is not part of the initial fall 2026 pipeline. A broader pathway may be developed later.

## Curriculum

Training content must reflect [[Values]], the confirmed City Group model, approved role boundaries, safeguarding and escalation requirements, and the Belonging–Beholding–Becoming framework. Specific sessions remain pending.

## Schedule

Training follows the July 28, August 11, and August 25 Team Night sign-up and discernment period and precedes final group assignments and commissioning. Exact dates and completion requirements are not yet confirmed.
