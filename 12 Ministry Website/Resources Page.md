## Before Publishing

Check each resource before it goes on the page:

- **Audience:** is it for anyone, or for leaders only? Leader guides carry policies, mandatory-reporting guidance, and leader agreements meant for leaders.
- **Public language:** the Belonging, Beholding, Becoming language is for group leader training only for now and is not published on the website (Paul, October 1, 2026). Resources that use it stay with leaders until that direction changes.
- **Third-party material:** CoJourner comes from The Garden Project Ministry. Describe it and link to the source; republishing its material needs attribution and permission ([[06 Resources/External Adaptations/Highlands Resource Adaptation\|External Adaptations]] shows the copyright boundary).
- **Approval:** a resource marked as awaiting pastoral review (for example, the draft group leader policies) is not published until Russ approves it.
- **Privacy:** no participant names, prayer requests, or locations, as on every Groups page (DEC-028).
