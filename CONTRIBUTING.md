# Contributing to Golden City Groups

Thank you for helping develop the Groups and Classes ministry resources of Golden City Church.

This repository contains ministry systems, leader resources, operational documents, and formation materials. Contributions should strengthen clarity, usability, theological faithfulness, pastoral care, and healthy ministry practice.

## Ways to Contribute

Helpful contributions may include:

* Correcting spelling, grammar, formatting, or broken links.
* Improving instructions for leaders and volunteers.
* Clarifying workflows, checklists, forms, and playbooks.
* Suggesting discussion questions or formation practices.
* Identifying gaps, inconsistencies, or outdated information.
* Improving accessibility and document organization.
* Sharing feedback from approved ministry pilots.

## Before Making Changes

Before editing a document:

1. Read the relevant README and surrounding files.
2. Confirm that your change fits the purpose of the folder.
3. Avoid duplicating an existing resource.
4. Preserve the ministry framework of **Belonging, Beholding, and Becoming**.
5. Ask a ministry leader before changing governance, theology, safeguarding, or pastoral-care policies.

## Sensitive Areas

The following changes require leadership review:

* Mission, vision, values, or ministry philosophy.
* Theology or discipleship frameworks.
* Safeguarding and child-protection procedures.
* Pastoral care, counseling, discipline, or crisis response.
* Leadership qualifications or authority structures.
* Data collection, privacy, attendance, or member records.
* Public communication representing Golden City Church.

## Privacy and Security

Never commit:

* Private pastoral notes.
* Counseling records.
* Personal phone numbers or addresses.
* Attendance lists containing identifiable information.
* Information about children or vulnerable adults.
* Passwords, API keys, tokens, or credentials.
* Confidential staff or leadership discussions.

Use fictional or clearly marked sample data in templates.

## File Standards

Use:

* Clear and descriptive file names.
* Markdown whenever practical.
* Short headings and readable paragraphs.
* Relative links for files within the repository.
* ISO dates in operational records: `YYYY-MM-DD`.
* Status labels such as `Draft`, `Under Review`, `Approved`, or `Archived`.

Avoid renaming or moving many files without explaining the reason.

## Suggested Workflow

1. Create a new branch.
2. Make one focused set of changes.
3. Review the changed files.
4. Commit with a clear message.
5. Open a pull request explaining:

   * What changed.
   * Why it changed.
   * Which ministry area is affected.
   * Whether leadership review is required.

Example commit messages:

```text
docs(groups): clarify city group leader responsibilities
fix(forms): remove collection of unnecessary personal data
feat(training): add new leader onboarding checklist
```

## Review Principles

Contributions will be evaluated according to whether they are:

* Biblically faithful.
* Christ-centered.
* Formation-oriented.
* Pastorally responsible.
* Practical for leaders.
* Appropriate for Golden City Church.
* Safe for public inclusion.

Submission does not guarantee acceptance.

## Contributor Agreement

By submitting a contribution, you confirm that:

* You created the contribution or have permission to submit it.
* It does not knowingly violate another person’s copyright or privacy.
* Golden City Church may edit, publish, and distribute the accepted contribution as part of its ministry resources.
* Your contribution may remain publicly accessible through this repository.

Thank you for helping build communities where people belong, behold Jesus, and become faithful disciples.
