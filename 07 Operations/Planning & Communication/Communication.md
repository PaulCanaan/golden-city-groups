Template wording is operational copy, not a leadership appointment or readiness decision. Send a message only when its trigger is true and its variables are confirmed. The Groups Director owns these communications; Paul is the current sender unless ownership changes.

## Communication Principles

Group Leader communication should be:

- warm, relational, pastoral, encouraging, and concise;
- clear about the next step without sounding like corporate HR;
- grounded in discipleship and participation in the local church;
- honest about what has and has not been approved; and
- personal enough to sound like it came from a ministry leader, not an automated organization.

Use relational language with prospective leaders: **Group Leader Conversation**, **grab coffee**, **leading or hosting**, **explore**, and **discern next steps together**. Reserve **Interviewed**, **Workflow Card**, **workflow stage**, **intake**, **pipeline**, and **advancement** for internal Planning Center operations.

## Template Standards

- Replace every bracketed variable before sending.
- Omit any variable or detail line that does not apply.
- Use the person's actual intake source only when it helps the message feel personal.
- Keep one main action in each message.
- Do not imply appointment, training completion, assignment, or launch readiness before it is recorded through the approved process.
- Keep sensitive pastoral, safeguarding, screening, or discernment details out of email, text, and general Planning Center notes.
- Use neutral placeholders; Planning Center merge-field syntax is not documented in this repository and must be confirmed during live configuration.

Standard placeholders:

`[First Name]` · `[Group Name]` · `[Date]` · `[Time]` · `[Location]` · `[Duration]` · `[Meeting Option 1]` · `[Meeting Option 2]` · `[Meeting Option 3]` · `[What to Bring / Preparation]` · `[Group Start Date]` · `[Planning Center / Public Listing]` · `[Leader Resources Link]` · `[Resource or Update]`

## Step 0 — Interest Submitted

Submission through the `Lead a Group` form or the group-leading option on the `Serve Team Interest Form` triggers the same workflow. Acknowledgment confirms receipt and names the next step; it does not invite the person into training or imply appointment.

### Email — Interest Acknowledgment

- **Stage:** Step 0 — Interest Submitted
- **Channel:** Email
- **Purpose:** Acknowledge interest and set the expectation that the Groups Director will follow up.
- **Trigger:** The person enters the Group Leader workflow through either approved intake path.
- **Owner:** Groups Director
- **Variables:** `[First Name]`, intake-source opening
- **Status:** Canonical

**Subject:** Thanks for your interest in leading a group

Hi [First Name],

**Choose one opening:**

- **Lead a Group form:** Thanks for filling out the Lead a Group form. I'm grateful that you're willing to explore leading or hosting a group at Golden City.
- **Serve Team Interest Form:** Thanks for filling out the Serve Team Interest Form and expressing interest in leading a group as a way to get more involved at Golden City Church.

Thank you for your willingness to serve and take this first step.

I'll follow up soon to invite you to grab coffee for a Group Leader Conversation. It will be a chance to hear your story, talk about your heart for community, and discern next steps together.

Paul<br>
Groups Ministry<br>
Golden City Church

### Text — Interest Acknowledgment

- **Stage:** Step 0 — Interest Submitted
- **Channel:** Text
- **Purpose:** Confirm receipt and make the follow-up personal.
- **Trigger:** The person enters the Group Leader workflow and a text is appropriate.
- **Owner:** Groups Director
- **Variables:** `[First Name]`
- **Status:** Canonical

> Hey [First Name], this is Paul from Golden City. Thanks for expressing interest in leading a group. I'm grateful for your willingness to serve, and I'll follow up soon about grabbing coffee for a Group Leader Conversation.

### Planning Center — Form Confirmation

- **Stage:** Step 0 — Interest Submitted
- **Channel:** Planning Center form confirmation
- **Purpose:** Confirm receipt without implying advancement or appointment.

- **Owner:** Groups Director
- **Variables:** None

## Related Ministry Communication Guidance

### Initial Leadership Language

- Describe leader interest as the beginning of discernment, not appointment.
- For Fall 2026, keep initial City Group leadership within the Launch Team boundary in DEC-018.
- Do not imply that Launch Team participation, a conversation, training attendance, or Planning Center access guarantees appointment or assignment.
- Preserve room for a later public leadership pathway without promising its timing or form.

### Channels

- **Direct email and text:** preferred for personal leader follow-up.
- **Planning Center confirmation:** may acknowledge form receipt once live behavior is tested.
- **Planning Center email or chat:** use only within approved permissions, visibility, moderation, and safeguarding boundaries.
- **Public communication:** may explain participant interest and approved offerings; it should not imply an immediate public path into Fall 2026 City Group leadership.

Channel ownership, backup coverage, and response expectations remain open where not already assigned.

### Planning Center Communication Procedure

- Personalize the canonical message for the person's actual stage.
- Confirm recipients, variables, links, dates, times, locations, and status before sending.
- Record the completed action and next step without copying the full message into the Workflow Card.
- Explain whether a person is expressing interest, requesting to join, registering, or joining; do not blur those statuses.
- Link to the specific approved offering or group-type page rather than a generic destination when possible.
- Keep sensitive pastoral, safeguarding, and discernment details out of email, text, chat, event notes, and this repository.
- Use event reminders only for confirmed events.
- Correct outdated public links when a group is duplicated, archived, closed, or deferred.
