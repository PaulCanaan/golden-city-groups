# Ministry Stories

## Document Status

**Draft stewardship process within approved minimum data boundaries; collection and publication rules remain open.** Do not store identifiable stories here until consent, review, storage, and publication practices are approved under PRO-030.

## Purpose

Notice and share truthful signs of God's work without exaggeration, pressure, or exposure of confidential pastoral information.

## Story Stewardship Questions

Before collecting or sharing a story, confirm:

- What happened, and what details are verified?
- Whose story is it, and have they freely consented to the intended audience and channel?
- Does the story disclose prayer, health, family, counseling, safeguarding, or other sensitive information?
- Could names, locations, dates, or circumstances identify someone who did not consent?
- Who reviews the story for pastoral care, theological accuracy, privacy, and communication fit?
- Can consent be withdrawn, and who removes or corrects the story?

## Minimum Non-Identifying Draft

- **Environment:** City Group / Bible Club / Class / Collective
- **Formation signal:** Belonging / Beholding / Becoming
- **Brief summary:** No names or sensitive details
- **Follow-up owner:** Approved ministry leader
- **Publication status:** Internal draft / approved channel / not for publication

This template is for planning only and should not be used to collect identifiable participant information before policy approval.
