# Candidate Next-Step Invitation

## Use

**Status:** Working communication template; training requirements, role assignments, and appointments remain approval-dependent.

**Owner:** Paul

**Trigger:** Send after the Group Leader Conversation only when the approved discernment outcome is to invite the candidate into training and the remaining readiness process.

**Boundary:** This is not an appointment message. Russ or his designated pastor retains appointment authority, and training or assignment alone does not authorize service as a leader or host.

## Email

**Subject:** Your next step toward leading a City Group

Hi [First Name],

Thanks again for taking the time to meet with me. I really enjoyed hearing more about your story and your heart for people and community.

After our conversation, I'm excited to invite you to move forward in the City Group [Leader/Host] process at Golden City!

You're not being asked to do this alone. Our goal is to equip and support you as you prepare to help build a healthy, Christ-centered community. We'll provide the structure, resources, training, and ongoing support you need along the way.

Your next steps are:

- [Complete leader training / attend leader gathering]
- [Confirm proposed group location, day, and time]
- [Complete any remaining Planning Center information]
- [Complete the remaining approval, assignment, and launch-readiness steps]

I'll walk with you through each of these steps, so don't feel like you need to figure everything out at once. Completing these steps does not by itself constitute appointment; we'll clearly confirm your role after the pastoral approval process is complete.

I'm grateful for your willingness to help build this ministry and excited to see what God does through your group.

Paul

Groups Ministry

Golden City Church
