# Leader Interest Acknowledgment

## Use

**Status:** Working communication template; exact wording is not separately approved.

**Owner:** Paul

**Trigger:** Send after a Launch Team member submits the Group Leader Interest Form.

**Boundary:** This message acknowledges interest and promises follow-up. It does not confirm training admission, assignment, approval, or appointment.

## Email

**Subject:** Thanks for your interest in leading a City Group!

Hi [First Name],

Thanks for taking the time to fill out the Group Leader Interest Form! I'm excited that you're interested in helping create a place where people can build relationships, grow in their faith, and follow Jesus together.

Our next step is simple: I'd love to sit down with you over coffee and hear more about your story, your heart for community, and what leading or hosting a group could look like for you.

I'll reach out soon to find a time that works.

In the meantime, thank you for being willing to take this step. We're building this ministry together, and I'm grateful that you're considering being part of it.

Paul

Groups Ministry

Golden City Church
