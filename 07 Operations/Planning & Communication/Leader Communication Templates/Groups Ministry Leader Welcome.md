# Groups Ministry Leader Welcome

## Use

**Status:** Working communication template; exact wording is not separately approved.

**Owner:** Paul

**Trigger:** Send after the person has received an explicitly recorded appointment and is entering active leader development and support.

**Boundary:** Confirm the recipient's approved role before sending. Do not use this message to imply an unresolved assignment, group, location, schedule, or launch-readiness decision.

## Email

**Subject:** Welcome to Groups Ministry at Golden City

Hi [First Name],

Welcome to Groups Ministry at Golden City!

We believe following Jesus was never meant to be a solo journey. Groups create spaces where people can belong, grow in their faith, care for one another, and practice following Jesus together.

As a group leader, you don't have to be the person with all the answers. Your role is to help create an environment where people can build meaningful relationships, engage Scripture, pray for one another, and take their next steps with Jesus.

And you're not doing that alone.

### Resources

We'll provide discussion guides and other resources to help you lead your gatherings. You don't need to create a Bible study from scratch every week.

### Training

We'll equip you with practical tools for facilitating conversations, caring for people, building healthy community, and navigating common challenges.

### Ongoing support

If something comes up in your group and you're not sure what to do, reach out. Part of my role is to support you, help you think through situations, and connect you with the right pastoral support when needed.

### Community with other leaders

You're part of a team. We want our group leaders to learn from one another, pray together, celebrate what God is doing, and grow together.

### A simple goal

You don't need to build the perfect group. Create a space where people can show up, be known, encounter Jesus, and grow together.

I'm grateful that you're stepping into this with us. As Golden City grows, groups will be one of the primary places where a crowd becomes a community—and where people learn to follow Jesus together.

Welcome to the team.

Paul

Groups Ministry

Golden City Church
