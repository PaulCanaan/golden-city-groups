## Planning process

For fall 2026, the primary objective is to recruit Launch Team members to host City Groups rather than conduct public recruitment:

1. Use the July 28, August 11, and August 25 Team Nights for vision, sign-ups, discernment, placement conversations, and preparation.
2. During the August Team Nights, Paul helps interested Launch Team members submit the Planning Center form, receives and organizes submissions, and emails applicants to arrange interviews and communicate immediate next steps.
3. Use one standardized interview stage and leader assessment workflow owned by Paul; the format, records, and role-specific criteria remain unresolved.
4. Train invited candidates.
5. Confirm group assignments, hosts or locations, capacities, and readiness.
6. Commission approved leaders before they begin public responsibility.

Public leadership pathways may be developed in later ministry seasons.

Paul's intake and immediate follow-up responsibility does not confer final appointment authority. Interview standards, candidate qualifications, final approval, role assignment, training requirements, safeguarding review, appointment, and commissioning remain unresolved.

For Spring 2027, the primary objective is to expand intentional leader development and multiplication. Integrate the four-session [[CoJourner]] pathway without inventing cohort dates, curriculum content, or multiplication targets.
