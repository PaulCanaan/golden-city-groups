## Church Center App Card

**File:** ![[07 Operations/Church Center App Card.pdf]]

One US Letter page. It keeps the group cards' frame (Roboto, the gold header labels, gold step numbers, warm-white tiles, the "scan" panel with the QR code bottom right) and, at Paul's request on 2026-10-29, adds color: a sky-to-mint hero band in Church Center's own colors with the app icon, colored icons for the four sections, and a charcoal scan panel. The icon is the Church Center mark on a white tile, drawn from the vector artwork on Golden City's own Church Center setup page (the gradients and shapes are Church Center's, unchanged). Church Center's colors appear only around the app; the rest of the card stays in the Groups palette. The Golden City Church logo, the same image the church uses as its Church Center avatar, sits beside the intro so people know what to look for in the app's church search (added and then moved there at Paul's request, 2026-10-29). The scan panel keeps at least 22pt of padding on every side; its last line sits 26pt above the panel's bottom edge.

**QR destination:** `https://goldencitychurchco.churchcenter.com/setup`, Golden City Church's own Church Center app setup link. On a phone it shows **App Store** and **Google Play** buttons, and after install the app offers to connect to Golden City Church without searching. Someone who already has the app is taken straight to Golden City Church.

**Card text (as drafted, revised with the humanizer skill on 2026-10-29):**

- **Hero:** Free for iPhone and Android · Get the Church Center app · Groups, signups, and giving in one place
- **Intro (beside the Golden City Church logo):** Golden City Church uses Church Center for life together during the week. In the app you can find a group, ask to join, see when and where it meets, and message the people in it. Church news still comes by email, so the app is optional.
- **Set it up:**
  1. Scan the code below with your phone's camera.
  2. Tap App Store or Google Play and install Church Center.
  3. Open the app and tap Get started. If it asks to use Golden City Church's link, tap yes. Otherwise, search for Golden City Church, choose the one with our logo, and tap This is my church.
  4. Enter your mobile number or email, then the six-digit code Church Center sends you.
- **In the app:**
  - **Groups:** Find a group and tap Request to Join. Members see its details and can chat.
  - **Signups:** Register for church events when signups are open.
  - **Give:** Give once or set up recurring gifts, and see your giving history.
  - **Your profile:** Keep your contact information current so your group leaders can reach you.
- **Scan panel:** Scan to get the app. Point your camera at the code. Golden City Church is already selected. On a computer: goldencitychurchco.churchcenter.com. Church website: goldencity.church.

**Why these four sections:** on 2026-10-29 Golden City Church's public Church Center showed **Give, Groups, and Signups** in its navigation; the profile comes with logging in. If more sections are published later (Calendar, Media, and so on), update the card. The section names follow Church Center's own labels so people can match the card to the app.

## Verification

- **QR code (2026-10-29):** decoded from the finished PDF at three render scales with ZXing; one code on the page, and it reads exactly the setup link above.
- **Destination (2026-10-29):** the setup link opened in a phone-sized browser and showed Church Center's setup page with App Store and Google Play buttons; on a desktop browser it falls through to the church's Church Center home page.
- **Not yet tested (BWR-009):** the full path on a real iPhone and a real Android phone: scan, install, Get started, the offer to use the church's link, and login. Do this once before printing, then record the date here.

## Printing

Same as the group cards: US Letter, portrait, **actual size / 100%** (not "fit to page"), color, best quality; heavier or matte paper reduces glare in the acrylic stand. Content sits at least 0.55 inch inside every edge. Test the printed QR code with a phone before setting the card out.
