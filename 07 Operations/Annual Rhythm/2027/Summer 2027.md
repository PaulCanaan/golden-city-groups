# Summer 2027

## Document Status

**June–August seasonal direction confirmed; offerings and exact dates open.** Russ approved a lighter summer posture on October 26 (DEC-080): expect travel-related lower commitment and encourage more activity-based group life. The church will still offer some summer classes; particular classes, leaders, and dates are not yet named.

## Intended Purpose

Support continuing community and formation at a sustainable pace during travel season. Activities can help people stay connected; community, prayer, and Scripture remain the shared baseline (DEC-083). Align actual group plans and any church summer classes with pastoral needs, leader capacity, and the church calendar.

## Required Inputs

- fall 2026 and spring 2027 reviews
- church-wide summer calendar and facility constraints
- confirmed three-branch priorities for summer
- offering owners, approved leaders, locations, capacity, safeguarding, and participant paths
- a clear decision on whether each environment should continue, pause, or use a lighter rhythm

## Planning Questions

- What form of community and formation is pastorally needed during summer?
- Which rhythms are sustainable for leaders and participants?
- Which summer classes will the church offer, and what Groups support and readiness work will they need?
- What must be approved before communication begins?
