## Document Status

**Seasonal planning guidance with mixed confirmation status.** Existing Fall 2026 and Spring 2027 direction is retained; dates, assignments, safeguarding, appointment, and readiness require their actual evidence and approvals.

## Purpose and Ownership

Translate confirmed ministry direction into a sustainable semester of belonging, Scripture-centered formation, and faithful service. **Owner:** Paul coordinates routine planning; Russ retains the applicable pastoral, appointment, and final readiness decisions. **Start:** preparation for a semester. **Finish:** approved offerings, actual dates, responsibilities, communication, and unresolved dependencies have canonical homes.

## Close the outgoing season

Use an existing [[07 Operations/Workflows/Biweekly Ministry Review|biweekly review]] for the seasonal handoff. Start from the outgoing season note, approved group records, and leader feedback.

1. Confirm what met, what is still awaiting its first gathering, and what was skipped or rescheduled. Record confirmed outcomes and known gaps on the relevant calendar or season note. Ask Paul whether each overdue event affects a person, an appointment, or a current offering. If there is no ongoing consequence, preserve the known facts and unknowns in the dated record and close the queue item with the reason.
2. Have a prayerful conversation with leaders. Ask what helped people belong, attend to Christ in Scripture and prayer, and act on what they received. Ask what support the leader needs and whether their role or capacity has changed. Use the [[07 Operations/Workflows/Leader Support Check-In|check-in workflow]] and [[07 Operations/Metrics|formation and participation measures]].
3. Give each offering an explicit disposition: **Continue**, **Change**, **Pause**, **Close**, or **Pending evidence/decision**. Record one accountable owner, the evidence or decision, next action and date, and who will tell participants where applicable. Keep individual details and the status of specific waiting City Groups in the approved system. The season note may show aggregate status and non-sensitive exceptions.
4. Preserve the dated season plan, issued resources, decision history, and actual outcomes. Carry consequential unresolved work in the existing queue. When closing a historical review question, record the reason and any unknowns. See [[08 Archive/Archive Overview|Archive Overview]].

## Prepare the next season

1. Recenter on the [[01 Governance/Ministry Model|three ministry branches]] and the [[07 Operations/Annual Rhythm/Annual Calendar|seasonal rhythm]]. Start with the people and leaders actually available. Record confirmed event dates on the [[00 Dashboard/Calendar|Calendar]].
2. Review returning leaders and changed assignments through the seasonal disposition. Use the [[07 Operations/Workflows/Leader Conversation Guide|Group Leader Conversation]] and [[07 Operations/Workflows/Planning Center Groups#3. Move prospective leaders from intake to launch|leader intake workflow]] for new candidates. When a seasonal opening makes sense, record who can express interest, when and where, and Paul's follow-up responsibility. Use the existing DEC-042 routes for interested congregants (DEC-091). Check preparation, safeguarding, appointments, permissions, and [[06 Resources/Leader Development/Leader Training#Leader Acknowledgements|Leader Acknowledgements]] for new and returning leaders.
3. Use [[07 Operations/Workflows/Group Launch Readiness#Offering Readiness Checks|Offering Readiness Checks]] for each proposed offering before public enrollment or launch. Give each proposed or returning offering its own row on the [[00 Dashboard/Launch Readiness Dashboard|Launch Readiness Dashboard]]. Record its accountable owner, next gate, evidence, and milestone. Review changed details and test the participant route before promotion. Russ or his designated pastor gives final readiness approval under DEC-021.
4. Align profiles, Church Center settings, public listings, and calendar records. Keep personal welcome and participant follow-through with the assigned owner. Use the [[07 Operations/Workflows/First Meetings Checklist|First Meetings Checklist]] and start leader support when the group actually meets.
5. Record what happened and what needs follow-through at the next existing biweekly review. Proposed offerings, training dates, and commitments stay pending until the applicable authority confirms them.

The [[07 Operations/Annual Rhythm/2027/Spring 2027|Spring 2027 plan]] carries the current handoff. The [[07 Operations/Annual Rhythm/2026/Fall and Winter 2026|Fall and Winter 2026 record]] preserves the church-plant-specific context.

## Seasonal Operations Reviewer

When a season is ending or a launch is approaching, use this role within the [[07 Operations/Workflows/Biweekly Ministry Review|Biweekly Ministry Review]]. Read the outgoing season record, relevant decisions, non-sensitive leader feedback, the queue, and the launch checklist. Prepare:

- outgoing offering dispositions and remaining person or offering follow-up
- one incoming readiness row per offering under consideration, with evidence or a named dependency
- the few decisions Paul or Russ needs to make at the review, with the date or milestone each affects

Paul supplies outcomes and sets operational next steps. Russ retains pastoral decisions. Record what the existing review confirms and carry unresolved items through its queue. Use the existing Planning Center Operations skill for system reconciliation and the [[07 Operations/Workflows/Leader Conversation Guide#Leader Conversation support role|Leader Conversation support role]] for interview preparation.
