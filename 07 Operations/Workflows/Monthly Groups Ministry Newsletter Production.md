# Monthly Groups Ministry Newsletter Production

## Document Status

Working procedure for the [[06 Resources/Leader Development/Monthly Groups Ministry Newsletter|monthly leader newsletter]] under DEC-093. Paul owns the issue and delivery. Send on the first Saturday at 8:00 a.m. Mountain Time once the issue and Planning Center audience are confirmed. Use the existing [[07 Operations/Workflows/Ministry Communication Workflow|Ministry Communication Workflow]] for delivery.

## Church Events Researcher

Run this role while planning each issue. Look for upcoming church-wide events, Groups & Classes updates, and invitations that group leaders may want to share. Focus on the period before the next issue. Include later events when leaders need advance notice or registration.

1. Start with the staff-meeting notes, internal updates, Sunday announcements, and content ideas Paul shares. Use those to set the issue's priorities and choose what matters to leaders. Record the source and distinguish a reported plan from a confirmed date. Check ministry dates against the [[00 Dashboard/Calendar|Groups calendar]] and approved season plan.
2. Use [Golden City Church's homepage announcements](https://www.goldencity.church/), [Events page](https://www.goldencity.church/events), [Groups page](https://www.goldencity.church/groups), and recent posts from official GCC social accounts to verify public details and catch relevant announcements. Prefer the specific event page, registration page, or post when available. Flag conflicting, stale, or missing details for Paul. Share only internal details he confirms are ready for group leaders.
3. Return a short list of items relevant to leaders, with one entry per event. Capture the event name, date and time when confirmed, one sentence on why it matters, and the public link or staff source. Include the location, sign-up link, or deadline only when it helps leaders act. Skip routine listings, unrelated ministry posts, and personal information.
4. Hand the shortlist to the issue drafter and Paul in the [[10 Templates/Communication/Monthly Groups Ministry Newsletter Issue#Event Research Notes|issue research notes]]. Label church-wide outreach and prayer or fasting opportunities as invitations that leaders and their groups can choose to join.

Examples include the Christmas Candlelight service, a church outreach opportunity open to groups, and Russ's planned January 21 days of prayer and fasting. For prayer and fasting, use Paul's report as an early input and confirm the dates and any participation details from the Sunday announcement or another official source before including them.

## Prepare and Send an Issue

1. **Plan.** Review Paul's latest internal updates and content ideas. Use the [[06 Resources/Leader Development/Groups Ministry Newsletter Content Calendar|Newsletter Content Calendar]] to place them in the appropriate issue. Use the [[#Church Events Researcher|Church Events Researcher]] to prepare the church-wide and Groups & Classes updates. Choose one short article that equips leaders this month.
2. **Draft.** Copy the [[10 Templates/Communication/Monthly Groups Ministry Newsletter Issue|issue template]]. For a launch issue, use its launch checklist. Write to the leaders as people Paul knows. Check dates, resource links, and ministry claims. Leave participant identities, care details, and leader records out of the issue.
3. **Review.** Give Paul the subject, message, links, and proposed timing. Record his approval of the issue and confirm authorization before sending.
4. **Verify the audience and send.** In Planning Center, verify the current serving leaders and any relevant role assignments with Paul. Check for stale or duplicate recipients and keep applicants and general participant lists outside the audience. Verify the sender, reply destination, and any merge fields in the preview. Send the approved issue through Planning Center.
5. **Record the result.** Record the actual send date and any non-sensitive follow-up in the issue note. Use the existing [[00 Dashboard/Biweekly Review Queue|review queue]] for unresolved operational work with an owner and next action.

Use the annual calendar and Collaboration notes to find confirmed milestones and resource themes. Personal care and urgent pastoral matters follow their existing channels.
