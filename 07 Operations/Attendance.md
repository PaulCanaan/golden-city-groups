# Attendance

## Document Status

**Proposed launch procedure; participant-data and pastoral-care controls await approval.** Planning Center Groups supports event attendance and leader reminders, but PRO-026 must be resolved before this procedure becomes governing.

## Purpose

Attendance helps leaders notice who was present, welcome visitors, maintain accurate group records, and recognize when a personal follow-up may be appropriate. It does not measure a person's faithfulness, spiritual maturity, or value to the church.

## Weekly Procedure

1. The assigned leader or approved attendance recorder confirms that the correct Planning Center event exists.
2. After the gathering, record leaders, members, approved visitors, and applicants using the applicable Planning Center roles.
3. Add a visitor to the roster only through the approved participant-data process.
4. Record attendance promptly within the response window established by the ministry owner.
5. Use an event note only for brief, non-sensitive operational context such as a cancellation or weather change.
6. Contact a person relationally when absence, a first visit, or a request suggests appropriate follow-up; do not automate pastoral conclusions from attendance.
7. Escalate care, abuse, crisis, or safety concerns through the church-approved process rather than recording details in attendance notes, chat, or this repository.

## Access and Privacy Boundaries

- Attendance access is limited to approved leaders, attendance recorders, group type managers, and administrators who need it for their role.
- Granting attendance permission does not grant leadership authority.
- Do not record counseling notes, identifiable prayer requests, health details, safeguarding reports, or discernment judgments in event notes.
- Do not export attendance with personal details into this repository.
- Children under 13 require the church-approved Check-Ins and safeguarding process rather than a general adult Groups workflow.
- Correct inaccurate records through the assigned Planning Center owner.

## Review Rhythm

The ministry owner should review aggregate completion and participation patterns at an approved rhythm. The review may surface questions for leaders, but personal follow-up remains relational and pastoral. Aggregate reporting belongs in [[07 Operations/Metrics]]; identifiable care details do not.
