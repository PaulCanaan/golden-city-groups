## Two Distinct Intake Purposes

### Group leader interest

Either intake path triggers the same Group Leader workflow:

1. The person submits the `Lead a Group` form; or
2. the person submits the `Serve Team Interest Form` and selects the group-leading option.

Submission is **Step 0 — Intake Trigger**, not an official workflow stage. The Groups Director receives the notification and/or Workflow Card and moves the person into **Welcome Email / Call**. The canonical stage definitions, actions, and completion conditions are in [[Planning Center Groups#3. Move prospective leaders from intake to launch|Planning Center Groups]].

### Public participant enrollment

Russ owns this system, with Paul as backup. Offering-specific notification and follow-up assignments still must be tested and recorded.

For an approved, launch-ready offering:

1. Direct the person to the tested offering-specific Church Center page.
2. Use the approved enrollment method and fields.
3. Send a confirmation that states what will happen next and does not imply placement until placement is complete.
4. Route the request to the named owner and backup.
5. Review capacity, fit, age or safeguarding requirements, and any necessary pastoral conversation.
6. Accept, place, waitlist, redirect, or decline the request pastorally.
7. Confirm the outcome and first meeting details.
8. Keep the Planning Center membership or request status current.

## Minimum Data Principle

Collect only information required for communication, placement, safety, and approved ministry follow-up. Do not copy participant records into this repository or personal spreadsheets. Sensitive pastoral information belongs in the church-approved pastoral or safeguarding process, not in a general join form or group note.
