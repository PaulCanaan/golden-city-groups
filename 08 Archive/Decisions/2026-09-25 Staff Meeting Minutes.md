### 2 — City Group Leader Design Freedom

City Group leaders have creative freedom to design their groups, including:

- **Frequency:** weekly or biweekly.
- **Day and time:** the day of the week and the meeting time.
- **Resource:** the Weekly Recap, a book of the Bible, a Christian book, a devotional, or another resource the leader prefers.
- **Group features:** descriptive labels such as young adults, young couples, or single moms. Labels help people find a group that fits them; they do not restrict who may join.

City Groups need not begin meeting immediately after Group Launch Sunday; a leader may begin one or two weeks later. The first several gatherings should focus mainly on getting to know the people who have joined. Paul will communicate these expectations to City Group leaders.

**Decision connection:** [[Decision History#DEC-052 — City Group Leader Design Freedom and Start Timing|DEC-052]]

Before Group Launch Sunday, Paul will finalize each group's details with its current leaders by email and text, then update the Groups webpage with the finalized information, including leader profile pictures and final meeting times and locations.

### 4 — Branch Formation Emphases

The ministry development pipeline confirms each branch's focus:

| Branch | Emphasis | Focus |
|---|---|---|
| **City Groups** | ~80% fellowship, ~20% discipleship | Breaking bread together and praying for one another. City Groups are unapologetically Christian community groups. They need not be in-depth Bible studies; they are community-based, and their priority is that people find belonging. |
| **Collectives** | ~50% fellowship, ~50% discipleship | Accessible gatherings where men and women walk alongside other men and women of God. Collective leaders and table leaders aim to equip participants to take part in discussion. A typical gathering opens with a 15–20 minute devotional followed by discussion at breakout tables. Collectives should become places where people find mentorship and lasting connection. |
| **Bible Clubs** | ~20% fellowship, ~80% discipleship | This fall the first curriculum, *Planted.*, begins. It is seeker- and new-believer-friendly, though everyone can learn from it. |

For Bible Clubs:

- *Planted.* will recur every fall.
- In Spring 2027, the following curriculum may be *Covenant, Kingdom, and People of God*.
- People who complete *Planted.* may be considered to lead it in the future.

**Decision connections:** [[Decision History#DEC-053 — Branch Formation Emphases and Collective Gathering Shape|DEC-053]]; [[Decision History#DEC-054 — Bible Club Curriculum Sequence|DEC-054]]
