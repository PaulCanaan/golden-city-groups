### 3 — First Group Leader Collaboration

Russ approves the Group Leader Collaboration and really likes the idea. The first gathering for all current group leaders is **Friday, October 30, 2026**, a dinner hosted by Paul at his home. Its future rhythm (biweekly or monthly), dates, and location are still to be decided.

- **First gathering's purpose:** check in with group leaders one month into the groups' launch: how are you, and how are your groups doing?
- **Design:** it is all about supporting group leaders. Leaders share their experiences and struggles and learn from one another. It is also a place where leaders are poured into and equipped.
- **Possible content:** the Belonging, Beholding, Becoming formation framework, the CoJourner Program, or other workshops.

**Decision connection:** implementation update to [[Decision History#DEC-056 — Group Leader Collaborations|DEC-056]]

### 4 — The Complexity Gate (Paul's Design Principle)

How deep should Bible study go in groups and classes? Most people don't find glory in complexity, and study content that is too deep can overwhelm. Paul's pedagogical principle is to keep participants in neither the comfort zone (too shallow) nor the danger zone (too deep), but in the adventure zone, which is exciting and challenges growth the most. Paul will later refactor and redesign the Galatians study by applying the Complexity Gate.

**Record:** [[06 Resources/Curriculum Development/Complexity Gate|Complexity Gate]], and part of [[Decision History#DEC-071 — Groups Resource Design Standard|DEC-071]]

### 5 — Email First; Promote the Church Center App

Not everyone has the Church Center app. Most of the Launch Team and all group leaders use it, and its group chats are convenient, but most church-goers may not know about it. As a result, some people who joined groups or classes did not receive updates unless their group leaders emailed them. Russ and Paul decided:

1. Keep email as the main method of communication. Do not expect everyone to be on the Church Center app.
2. Promote the Church Center app with a table card like the group table cards. People scan a QR code to download the app. The card includes instructions for connecting to Golden City Church and a brief introduction to each section of the app.

**Decision connections:** [[Decision History#DEC-073 — Email as the Primary Participant Channel|DEC-073]] and [[Decision History#DEC-074 — Standing Lobby Table Cards|DEC-074]]

### 6 — Groups & Classes Ministry Card

Besides the Church Center card, the ministry will make a general card for GCC Groups & Classes Ministry, where people can learn about the ministry, find a group to join, sign up to lead a group, and similar. The group table cards (specific group profiles) are used only for group launches; the Church Center app card and the ministry card can be presented in the lobby all the time.

**Decision connection:** [[Decision History#DEC-074 — Standing Lobby Table Cards|DEC-074]]

### 7 — Groups No One Has Joined Yet

*Supplied by Paul later on October 19.* The minutes name no specific groups. Recording this helps future group launches, because similar situations will likely happen again.

Two City Groups have no one signed up yet, probably because the people attending Sunday services and checking out groups do not live in those areas. Paul talked with those leaders. They understand the dynamic of a small church and are not discouraged by it.

- **Join another group meanwhile:** Russ will encourage these leaders to join other City Groups, so they don't miss out on community and group life while they wait for people to join theirs. Paul will also reach out and encourage them to join other groups first.
- **Their groups stay open:** they can start whenever people join.
- **Keep promoting:** Russ will keep promoting groups, especially those with more open spots, on social media and in stage announcements.

**Decision connection:** [[Decision History#DEC-075 — City Groups Waiting for Members|DEC-075]]
