## Decision 3 — Leader, Host, and Instructor Standards

**Related questions:** OQ-004 and OQ-005
**Launch priority:** Launch-blocking
**Approval status:** Partially resolved July 27, 2026; Planted direction recorded in DEC-023

### Theological and ministry rationale

Leadership is stewardship of people rather than recognition of status. Character, theological alignment, relational trust, teachability, capacity, and demonstrated faithfulness should be discerned alongside skill.

### Ministry purpose

Establish a minimum appointment and preparation standard for people who lead groups, host meetings, facilitate Planted, or exercise pastoral influence.

### Target participants

Prospective and approved leaders, hosts, instructors, apprentices, coaches if used, and participants entrusted to their care.

### Formation relationship

- **Belonging:** leaders cultivate hospitality, safety, care, and participation.
- **Beholding:** leaders handle Scripture faithfully and direct attention toward Christ.
- **Becoming:** leaders model character, service, repentance, and teachability while helping others grow.

### Recommended minimum launch standard

Before appointment, a leader, host, or instructor should have:

- A pastoral or delegated relational conversation
- Confirmed alignment with Golden City Church's mission, values, and beliefs
- A role description with authority boundaries
- Appropriate role-specific preparation
- Agreement to confidentiality, safeguarding, reporting, and escalation expectations
- Confirmation of capacity and availability for the fall rhythm
- Any church-required screening or background check
- An explicitly recorded appointment rather than appointment by interest or attendance alone

Correction, temporary pause, and removal authority should be named before launch. Apprentices may receive bounded, supervised responsibility but should not be represented as approved leaders until appointed.

### Additional Planted authorization

Russ should approve the fall instructor or facilitator and confirm that Planted has received sufficient pastoral and theological review for its intended audience. Authorship by Paul and Grant does not by itself establish teaching authorization.

### Operational requirements

- Approve role descriptions and authority boundaries.
- Name who conducts discernment conversations and records appointment.
- Identify role-specific training and any required screening.
- Name who may pause or remove a leader and how urgent concerns are escalated.
- Record the approved Planted instructor or facilitator.

## Decision 4 — Pastoral Care, Safeguarding, and Participant Data

**Related questions:** OQ-006 and OQ-012
**Launch priority:** Launch-blocking
**Approval status:** Awaiting pastoral approval

### Theological and ministry rationale

People are neighbors to love and persons entrusted to the church's care, not data points or ministry capacity. Faithful care requires both compassionate presence and clear limits, including prompt escalation when needs exceed a leader's role.

### Ministry purpose

Establish the church-approved rules for confidentiality, abuse or crisis reporting, ministry to minors and vulnerable people, home meetings, childcare, background checks, referrals, and handling participant information.

### Target participants

All participants and leaders, with particular attention to minors, vulnerable people, hosts, parents or guardians, and anyone sharing sensitive pastoral information.

### Formation relationship

- **Belonging:** hospitality and care are practiced without promising secrecy or safety the ministry cannot guarantee.
- **Beholding:** care remains truthful, prayerful, and accountable to Scripture and pastoral wisdom.
- **Becoming:** leaders learn faithful judgment, restraint, service, and appropriate referral.

### Recommended minimum launch boundaries

- Leaders do not promise absolute confidentiality.
- Imminent danger, suspected abuse, threats of harm, and other urgent concerns follow the church-approved escalation and reporting process immediately.
- General repository files do not contain names, rosters, contact information, identifiable prayer requests, counseling notes, or sensitive pastoral details.
- Church Center access is role-based and limited to information required for ministry responsibility.
- Planning Center Groups leaders receive only the access required for their approved group; wider People database search remains disabled unless expressly approved.
- Participant information is not copied into personal spreadsheets, notes, or messaging systems without explicit church approval.
- Chat, direct messaging, event notes, membership visibility, and public event visibility follow approved group-type settings rather than individual preference.
- Meetings involving minors, childcare, vulnerable people, or private homes do not begin until the applicable church requirements are confirmed.
- Group leaders offer prayer, presence, practical care, and pastoral connection within their role; they do not act as licensed counselors or sole crisis responders.

These are proposed ministry boundaries, not a substitute for church policy, legal counsel, mandated-reporting guidance, or emergency procedures.

### Operational requirements

- Provide or approve the governing church policies.
- Name the primary and backup escalation contacts.
- Define the urgent escalation method and expected response window.
- Define screening, supervision, check-in, transportation, and parent or guardian requirements for minors.
- Define what Church Center fields may be collected, who may access them, and how corrections or deletion requests are handled.
- Name Planning Center administrators, group type managers, join-request owners, and backups; approve leader permissions and the access-review rhythm.
- Approve the settings for member confidentiality, chat, direct messaging, event notes, event visibility, enrollment, capacity, and attendance access.
- Replace repository fields that imply local storage of rosters or identifiable prayer needs.

Belonging, Beholding, Becoming can help leaders hold together community, attention to Christ, and formation into Christlikeness. Public language should remain clear, church-aligned, and proportionate to what the ministry can actually explain and practice.

The whole church, newcomers, group and class participants, and leaders who will explain and embody the ministry's purpose.
