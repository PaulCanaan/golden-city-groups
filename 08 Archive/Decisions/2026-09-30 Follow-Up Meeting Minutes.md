## Outcomes

### 1 — Collective Schedules and Locations

Schedule and location details are normal to change, and the ministry should hold them with flexibility.

- **Women's Collective:** Miranda Daly and Rachel O'Brien found that Thursdays work better than Fridays. Women's Collective starts **Thursday, October 15, 2026**. The location will likely rotate between the Dalys' and the O'Briens' homes.
- **Men's Collective:** starts **Thursday, October 8, 2026**. For now it gathers at the Dalys' home.
- **Long-term direction:** Russ intends the Collectives to become on-site gatherings in the future, rather than meeting in homes the way off-site, location-based City Groups do. The ministry keeps watching for a potential location to host them.

**Decision connection:** [[Decision History#DEC-060 — Thursday Collective Schedules and On-Site Direction|DEC-060]]

### 2 — Group Dynamics

Some groups will be more popular than others, and that is okay. A group with few sign-ups or inconsistent attendance does not disqualify its leaders, and leaders should not be discouraged by it. For example, Women's Collective might have 40 people sign up and Men's Collective 2; that can be a normal dynamic for this church and ministry. The ministry keeps group dynamics in mind as it goes forward.

**Decision connection:** [[Decision History#DEC-061 — Uneven Group Dynamics Are Normal|DEC-061]]

### 3 — Likely *Planted.* Venue

Discovery Church in Broomfield has a church coffee shop, Barn Door Coffee, whose space is available on Tuesday evenings but not on Thursdays, which is why the Collectives are not hosted there. Russ has spoken with Discovery Church's pastor about the space, so *Planted.* will very likely meet there on Tuesday evenings. Paul will visit the coffee shop to confirm the Tuesday-evening space use.

**Status:** Likely arrangement, pending Paul's confirmation visit. Recorded as an operational update to BWR-011 and BWR-018, not a decision.

### 4 — City Groups as a Signature Ministry

Russ expressed his heart for City Groups. Because the church is called Golden City Church, he wants City Groups to be an important, recognizable feature of Golden City: when people hear "City Groups," they should think of Golden City's groups ministry. City Groups and the church share one vision and mission, to be in the city and for the city.

**Decision connection:** [[Decision History#DEC-062 — City Groups as a Signature Ministry of Golden City|DEC-062]]

### 5 — Leader Guides

The ministry should make a **City Group Guide** and a **Men's and Women's Collective Guide** so leaders serve together with a clear vision. The guides support leaders by helping them know what to expect, how to troubleshoot, and how to make a mandatory report in an emergency. They will include the mission, vision, and values of the Groups Ministry, leader agreements, and church policies.

**Decision connection:** [[Decision History#DEC-063 — City Group and Collective Leader Guides|DEC-063]]

### 6 — Group Leader Collaborations

Russ really likes the Group Leader Collaborations idea. Paul plans to host a dinner for all current leaders about one month after Group Launch Sunday, to hear how everyone's groups went. More details will be shared in the Planning Center group chat for all current leaders.

**Status:** Implementation update to [[Decision History#DEC-056 — Group Leader Collaborations|DEC-056]].
