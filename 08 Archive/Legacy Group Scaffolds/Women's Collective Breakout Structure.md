# Women's Collective Breakout Structure

## Document Status

**Blocked — awaiting an offering decision.** No breakout structure is approved or active for fall 2026.

## Intended Purpose

If future Women's Collective gatherings need smaller conversations, this page will define why breakouts are used, who facilitates them, how participants are assigned, what content is discussed, and how care or safeguarding concerns are escalated.

## Required Before Development

- confirmed need within the approved Women's Collective gathering plan
- appointed Collective leader and accountable breakout facilitators
- approved leader standards under DEC-027
- approved minimum care, confidentiality, safeguarding, and data boundaries under DEC-028
- location, room, capacity, accessibility, and participant-flow plan
- approved content and follow-up responsibility

Until these items exist, use no breakout structure merely because this file exists.
