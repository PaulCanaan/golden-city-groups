## Ministry Emphasis

Men's Collective is designed around approximately **50% fellowship and 50% structured discipleship**. Gathering design should give meaningful room to relationships, conversation, and mutual encouragement while also sustaining intentional engagement with Scripture, curriculum, prayer, and response. The ratio is a design emphasis, not a timed quota.
