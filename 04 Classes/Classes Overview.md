# Classes

## Document Status

**Bible Clubs are a current Groups Ministry branch.** Teacher-led Classes develop within that branch. Russ approved a Fall 2027 Freedom Class pilot for current group leaders (DEC-088). Specific operating details and readiness remain open.

## Current Focus — Bible Clubs

Bible Clubs are facilitator-led gatherings that study a book or section of Scripture. [[04 Classes/Curriculum/Planted Bible Study|Planted]] is the first confirmed Bible Club offering. Its curriculum is approved, while its operational readiness remains governed by the [[00 Dashboard/Launch Readiness Dashboard|Launch Readiness Dashboard]], [[07 Operations/Annual Rhythm/2026/Fall and Winter 2026|Annual Rhythm]], and Planning Center.

## Ministry Emphasis

Future Classes may become teacher-led, structured environments for topic-based equipping and disciple-maker development. A healthy learning community would support:

- **Intellectual formation:** deeper understanding of Scripture, theology, interpretation, and the Christian story.
- **Spiritual formation:** worship, prayer, repentance, obedience, freedom in Christ, service, and faithful practice.

Classes should be rigorous without becoming detached academic programs and spiritually formative without treating reflection or emotion as a substitute for careful learning.

The [[04 Classes/Curriculum/Freedom Class|Freedom Class]] pilot helps equip future facilitators. Alpha, Barna resources, and financial and marriage classes remain future ideas. Each offering needs an owner, instructor, curriculum review, operating plan, participant path, and readiness approval.
