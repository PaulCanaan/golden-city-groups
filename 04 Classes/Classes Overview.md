# Classes

## Document Status

**Approved future development path under Bible Clubs; no Class offering is currently active or approved for launch.** Planted is a Bible Club, not a Class.

## Ministry Emphasis

Future Classes may become teacher-led, structured environments for topic-based equipping and disciple-maker development. A healthy learning community would support:

- **Intellectual formation:** deeper understanding of Scripture, theology, interpretation, and the Christian story.
- **Spiritual formation:** worship, prayer, repentance, obedience, freedom in Christ, service, and faithful practice.

Classes should be rigorous without becoming detached academic programs and spiritually formative without treating reflection or emotion as a substitute for careful learning.

Financial classes and marriage classes are approved examples of possible future types, not approved offerings. Every future Class still requires an owner, teacher, curriculum review, operating plan, participant path, and readiness approval.
