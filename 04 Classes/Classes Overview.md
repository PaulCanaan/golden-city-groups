# Classes

## Document Status

**Confirmed ministry purpose with first offering approved in part.** Classes are the ministry's most concentrated environment for intellectual and spiritual formation. Paul is confirmed to lead the first Planted class as a nine- or ten-week course leading to Christmas; remaining curriculum and operational readiness requirements are still open.

## Ministry Emphasis

Classes are teacher-led, structured environments for topic-based equipping and disciple-maker development. A healthy learning community supports:

- **Intellectual formation:** deeper understanding of Scripture, theology, interpretation, and the Christian story.
- **Spiritual formation:** worship, prayer, repentance, obedience, freedom in Christ, service, and faithful practice.

Classes should be rigorous without becoming detached academic programs and spiritually formative without treating reflection or emotion as a substitute for careful learning.
