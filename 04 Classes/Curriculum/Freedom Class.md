# Freedom Class

## Document Status

Russ approved a Fall 2027 pilot for current group leaders on November 23, 2026 ([[08 Archive/Decisions/Decision History#DEC-088 — Fall 2027 Freedom Class Leader Pilot|DEC-088]]). Freedom Class is part of the Classes development path within Bible Clubs.

## Purpose and participants

Invite current group leaders to experience Freedom Class and grow in freedom in Christ. Shared learning supports belonging. Scripture and prayer help leaders behold Christ, and faithful response prepares some to serve others in a future class. Paul coordinates pilot planning under DEC-021. Future leaders follow the appointment and preparation process under DEC-027.

## Conference conclusion

Plan future runs so a Freedom Conference can be the final part of the class. Church of the Highlands is the model discussed at staff. Its [Freedom Conference page](https://freedom.churchofthehighlands.com/conference) describes the conference as the conclusion of its small-group journey. Confirm the conference host, dates, and format before planning around a specific event.

## Pilot preparation

Confirm the facilitator, cohort, dates, venue, materials and permissions, and applicable pastoral care, safeguarding, and readiness. Confirm how the pilot will equip future facilitators and how Russ will review readiness for any later public run. See the [[06 Resources/External Adaptations/Highlands Resource Adaptation#Freedom Groups Boundary|Highlands adaptation notes]] and [[00 Dashboard/Biweekly Review Queue|BWR-046]].
