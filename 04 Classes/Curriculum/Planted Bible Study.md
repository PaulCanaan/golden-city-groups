## Formation Goal

> Our prayer is that participants will:
>
> - be rooted in grace and truth;
> - learn to hear God through Scripture;
> - see their story within God's story;
> - behold and follow Jesus;
> - grow in faith over time;
> - belong to the people of God;
> - plant seeds of hope in the lives of others.
>

## Forms of Learning in Each Gathering

Each gathering weaves together several forms of learning and formation:

- **Scripture** — reading and studying the biblical text
- **Story** — locating each passage within the larger story of God
- **Experience** — activities, visual metaphors, art, and embodied learning
- **Conversation** — honest questions and shared discovery
- **Practice** — applying truth in everyday life
- **Prayer and worship** — responding to God personally and together
- **Community** — growing alongside others rather than alone

These seven elements carry real operating weight. Experiential activity and embodied learning need floor space, not rows of chairs; worship needs a way to play music; conversation needs a room where a circle can hear itself. The venue search in [[00 Dashboard/Biweekly Review Queue|BWR-018]] should be given these requirements rather than a generic room request.

## Recurring Frameworks

The overview names **five recurring frameworks** that return across the study. Session 0 introduces one of them:

### The Grace-Truth Matrix

- **Anchor text:** John 1:14 — "The Word became flesh and dwelt among us. We observed his glory, the glory as the one and only Son from the Father, full of grace and truth."
- **Definitions used:** *Truth* — what is objectively and universally real; facts, reality, God's Word. Truth does not depend on our belief. *Grace (charis)* — God's unearned favor; his empowering love given as gift. Grace is received, not achieved.
- **Supporting quotations:** Chris Hodges — "Truth without grace is mean, grace without truth is meaningless, but truth and grace together are good medicine." Timothy Keller — "To be loved but not known is comforting but superficial. To be known and not loved is our greatest fear. But to be fully known and truly loved is, well, a lot like being loved by God."

The Grace-Truth Matrix is introduced through the Four Relationships activity below, so participants experience the four quadrants before the framework is named.

**The remaining four frameworks are not recorded anywhere in this vault.** They live in the Notion publishing package. Recording their names here would let participant-facing communication and the session calendar be built from one source.

## Session 0 — Orientation: The Seed and the Soil

Session 0 is an orientation session. Before the study moves into Genesis and the garden, it begins with Jesus' parable about soil, because the same seed can be received in very different ways.

The deck's opening image sets the study's governing metaphor: *Planted.* tells the story of a seed — the seed of the Kingdom, the seed of Jesus, the seed of your faith.

> A seed may look small and ordinary, but it carries life within it. Once it enters the soil, something hidden begins. Roots reach downward before anything appears above the ground. Growth comes slowly, quietly, and often invisibly.

### Session shape

| Movement | Content |
|---|---|
| Opening metaphor | The seed and the soil; what *Planted.* is |
| Scripture | The Parable of the Sower — Matthew 13:3–9 |
| Experience | The Four Relationships activity |
| Framework | The Grace-Truth Matrix — John 1:14 |
| Return to Scripture | Matthew 13:10–13 and 13:18–23 — Jesus' own interpretation of the parable |
| Entering the story | The journey ahead and the honest invitation |
| Worship and prayer | "Tend" |
| Close | Next week — Session 1: In the Beginning, a Garden |

### The Four Relationships activity

The room is set up as a large square divided into four quadrants. Three contain activities; the fourth is intentionally left empty. For twenty minutes participants may move between the three active quadrants at will, but must keep doing that quadrant's activity wherever they are, and are asked to stay in one quadrant for at least five minutes.

- **Top left:** burpees or jumping jacks with perfect form and a perfect smile
- **Bottom left:** sit or squat, turn away, cover your ears, and disengage
- **Bottom right:** unlimited snacks and drinks, but you must keep eating or drinking to stay
- **Top right:** empty

When time is up, everyone stops and moves together into the empty quadrant. There, they do nothing. They rest.

**Operational implications for the venue and first session:**

- The activity needs an open floor large enough for four quadrants and free movement — a significant constraint on [[00 Dashboard/Biweekly Review Queue|BWR-018]]'s venue search.
- Physical exertion is one quadrant's activity. The session needs a stated alternative for participants who cannot or should not do burpees or jumping jacks, and the invitation should make clear that participation is genuinely optional.
- Snacks and drinks are required supplies, which raises allergen awareness and a possible participant cost that is still undocumented.

### Worship and prayer

- **Song:** "Tend" by Emmy Rose — © 2021 Bethel Music Publishing et al., CCLI #7172668
- The song is a prayer to be tended: the Gardener breaks up fallow ground, prunes what runs wild, and sustains what he has started through winter. It returns the session's soil metaphor to God as the one who works it.

Lyrics are not reproduced here. **Confirm Golden City Church's CCLI license covers this song for the Planted setting before it is projected or printed** — the CCLI number is recorded on the slide, but no license evidence exists in this repository.

### The honest invitation

> No one enters this story with perfect soil.
>
> We enter with questions, wounds, hopes, resistance, faith, doubt, and unfinished stories. The invitation is not to pretend otherwise. It is to come honestly and allow the Gardener to work.

This is the posture the participant-facing [[07 Operations/Planning & Communication/Planted Introduction Email|Planted Introduction Email]] should carry, and it is why the study is described as genuinely seeker-friendly rather than seeker-friendly in name.

## Formation Purpose

Planted is a discussion-based Bible Club that cultivates sustained attention to Scripture, shared observation and interpretation, prayer, and faithful response. It should hold intellectual and spiritual formation together while preserving the facilitator-led Bible Club model.
