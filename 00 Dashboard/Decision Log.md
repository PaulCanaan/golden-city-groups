# Active Decision Register

## Purpose

This is the working dashboard for decisions that currently govern ministry development or require staff direction. It is intentionally concise.

- Full confirmed-decision records are preserved in [[08 Archive/Decisions/Decision History|Decision History]].
- The reviewed July 27 brief is preserved in [[08 Archive/Decisions/2026-07-27 Pastoral Approval Brief|2026-07-27 Pastoral Approval Brief]].
- Meeting-ready proposals are developed in [[Staff Decision Brief|Staff Decision Brief]].

An item is not governing merely because it appears in the staff brief. Proposed items become confirmed only after staff or pastoral direction is recorded here and the full record is added to Decision History.

## Status Guide

- **Confirmed** — approved and governing
- **Proposed** — prepared for staff consideration; not governing
- **Open operational item** — Paul may develop a recommendation, but a named detail is still needed
- **Archived** — full evidence and history are preserved in Decision History

## Decision Workflow

1. Paul develops a recommendation in the Staff Decision Brief.
2. Staff discusses the proposal and records direction, revisions, date, and approver.
3. An approved proposal receives the next DEC number and is summarized in this register.
4. The full decision record is added to Decision History.
5. Related ministry and operational documents are updated.
6. Superseded or reviewed briefs move to the Archive; the active register remains short.

## Record Template

```markdown
### DEC-XXX — Title

- **Decision:** What was approved?
- **Status:** Confirmed
- **Date and approver:** Who approved it, and when?
- **Evidence:** What meeting, document, or direction supports it?
- **Implications:** What ministry work changes?
- **Boundary:** What does this not authorize or settle?
```
