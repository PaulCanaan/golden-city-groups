## Readiness Statuses

| Status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Ready** | Every stated criterion is supported by repository evidence, and any required approval is recorded. |
| **On Track** | Work has an owner and credible next step, no current dependency prevents progress, and available evidence supports the planned timing. |
| **In Progress** | Work has begun, but one or more stated criteria remain incomplete. |
| **Blocked** | The next required step cannot advance because a decision, approval, access, policy, person, or system dependency is missing. |
| **At Risk** | Work can advance, but timing, missing evidence, or an unresolved dependency could materially impair launch without intervention. |
| **Not Started** | The requirement applies, but the repository contains no evidence that implementation has begun. |
| **Not Applicable** | The requirement is outside the stated launch scope or explicitly deferred. |

`Ready` applies to the named deliverable only; it never implies that an entire offering is launch-ready.

## Critical Path to Launch

### September 20 Public Launch / City Groups

```text
Launch Team host interest
  → Paul receives and reconciles submissions
  → Paul follows up and schedules interviews
  → approved interview and assessment process
  → pastoral appointment decision
  → group/host/location/capacity assignment
  → approved minimum launch preparation
  → Planning Center group build and participant-path test
  → Paul recommends readiness
  → Russ or designated pastor records final approval
  → public listing and participant connection
  → approved City Group start window
```

Not every applicant advances. A form, interview, training attendance, assignment draft, or Planning Center role does not establish appointment.

### Parallel Work That Must Converge Before Publication

- **Governance and safety:** policies, escalation contacts, host/home safeguards, and final authority.
- **Systems:** participant path, ownership, settings, permissions, data practices, and testing.
- **Communication:** Squarespace access, accurate branch/offering content, reviewed CTAs, and working links.
- **Leader preparation:** minimum role expectations, care/escalation orientation, Planning Center use, and completion record.

Leader interest and follow-up can continue while these parallel tracks advance. Appointment, public listing, enrollment, and launch cannot bypass their applicable gates.
