- [ ] Confirm the launch dates, each offering's seasonal disposition, its accountable owner, and what can be announced.

- [ ] Record Russ's or his designated pastor's launch, develop, or defer disposition for each offering, with any conditions. Keep provisional listings closed or unlisted until the applicable approval is recorded.
- [ ] Align approved dates, profiles, and join links across Church Center, the Groups page, and any launch cards. Test the mobile experience and QR destinations where applicable.
- [ ] Confirm the promotion and launch-day plan, who will staff it, and what next step participants will receive. Use only the offerings and paths cleared for public use.

### First Gatherings and Follow-Up

- [ ] Hand off to [[07 Operations/Workflows/First Meetings Checklist|First Meetings]] and confirm how leaders will send details privately and follow up.
- [ ] Record actual first gatherings separately from publication and approval. Update the authorized group and leader records and hand off to [[07 Operations/Workflows/Leader Support Check-In|leader support]].
