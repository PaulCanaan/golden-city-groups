# Inbox

## Document Status

**Operational capture page; not a governing record or permanent archive.** Use this page for ministry information that has been received but not yet clarified and filed.

## Purpose and Rules

- Capture quickly.
- Clarify the item before filing it.
- Move each item to its proper destination.
- Do not leave resolved items permanently in the Inbox.
- Do not treat unverified notes as approved decisions, policy, assignments, or pastoral direction.
- Keep confidential pastoral, safeguarding, screening, and participant information out of this page.

The Inbox may temporarily hold conversation notes, Team Night observations, leader-interest follow-up items, Planning Center issues, ministry ideas, decision candidates, staff questions, resources to review, operational problems, unassigned tasks, feedback, risks, or external links awaiting evaluation. It is not a second [[00 Dashboard/Groups Ministry Dashboard|Groups Ministry Dashboard]], task-management system, or archive.

## Operating Boundaries

- **Inbox:** temporary capture and triage.
- **[[07 Operations/Biweekly Ministry Review|Biweekly Ministry Review]]:** sole recurring review workflow; it processes the Inbox and reconciles canonical sources.
- **[[00 Dashboard/Biweekly Review Queue|Biweekly Review Queue]]:** unresolved, review-worthy operational work carried between reviews.
- **[[00 Dashboard/Groups Ministry Dashboard|Groups Ministry Dashboard]]:** current priorities, readiness, and operational status.
- **[[00 Dashboard/Staff Decision Brief|Staff Decision Brief]]:** matters requiring staff discussion or direction.
- **[[00 Dashboard/Decision Log|Decision Log]]:** approved decisions and current governing direction.
