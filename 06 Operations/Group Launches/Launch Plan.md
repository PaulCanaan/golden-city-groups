# Fall 2026 City Group Launch Plan

## Document Status

**Operational plan with mixed confirmation status.** The Launch Team, Team Night dates, initial internal leadership pipeline, and September 20 public launch are confirmed. Leader names, training dates, assignments, commissioning details, group locations, and capacities remain pending.

## Purpose

Move prayerfully from the relationships already formed in the church plant to a small number of accountable, prepared, and truthfully communicated City Groups.

## Boundaries

- City Group leader sign-up is not appointment.
- Initial City Group leaders are not recruited from the general public.
- Launch Team participation does not guarantee leadership or a particular assignment.
- No geographic scaffold is active solely because a file exists.
- Public leadership pathways may be developed later and are not ruled out by this initial process.
- Existing approval, safeguarding, data, and readiness gates remain in force.
