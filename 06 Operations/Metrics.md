## Planning Center Use

- Use aggregate group membership, enrollment-request, capacity, event, and attendance data where it helps leaders notice operational needs.
- Review attendance completion separately from attendance totals; a missing report is an operational issue, not proof that no one gathered.
- Do not place names, contact details, identifiable prayer requests, counseling notes, or safeguarding concerns in ministry dashboards or this repository.
- Interpret trends with leaders and pastoral context. A number may prompt a conversation but should not make a pastoral judgment by itself.
- Define the reporting owner, review rhythm, retention practice, and approved audience before creating recurring reports or exports.

## Interpret Measures by Environment

Do not evaluate every environment as though it serves the same purpose.

- **City Groups:** notice belonging, relational connection, care, participation, and whether the weekly recap helps people carry the weekend message into life. Do not judge health by classroom-style content completion.
- **Men's and Women's Collectives:** notice both meaningful fellowship and engagement with the biblical or formative focus.
- **Classes:** notice intellectual understanding and spiritual response together. Content completion alone does not demonstrate formation, while relational warmth alone does not fulfill the class purpose.

The distinctions are interpretive guides, not numerical performance targets or public ministry language.
