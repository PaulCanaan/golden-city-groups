# Biweekly Ministry Review Report

## Document Status

**Canonical report structure transferred from the existing review procedure; not a completed review or ministry-readiness record.**

## Purpose and Use

Create a dated report in 09 Reports/Biweekly Reviews after inspecting the canonical sources. Record actual review execution and required human review separately from unresolved ministry work.

- **Owner:** Reviewer; Paul supplies ministry judgment and missing facts, and Russ retains pastoral decisions.
- **Trigger:** The existing first/third-Monday review or its due catch-up run.
- **Variables / inputs:** Review date, window, reviewer, previous/next review, and actual scheduled occurrence when automated.

## Dated Report Execution Layer

### Checkbox Meaning and Completion Rules

A checked box means the reviewer:

1. inspected the relevant canonical source;
2. reconciled material changes;
3. updated the canonical document or queue when needed; and
4. surfaced missing evidence or human input when the matter remained unresolved.

Checkboxes measure **review execution**, not ministry readiness. A ministry area may remain `Blocked` or `At Risk` after its review box is checked. Leave a box unchecked when the area was skipped. Do not use checkbox state to replace readiness terminology, a formal decision, or a queue status.

### Review Metadata

Every dated report records:

- **Review date**
- **Review window**
- **Reviewed by**
- **Review status:** `Not Started`, `In Progress`, or `Complete`
- **Previous review**
- **Next scheduled review**

An automated repository pass remains `In Progress` until its required human review is complete. State review-execution completion separately from unresolved ministry issues; open queue items do not prevent a faithfully executed review from becoming complete.

### Current Review Emphasis

Near the top, list three to five matters derived from launch-critical queue items, deadlines before the next review, high-impact blockers, past events needing reconciliation, or decisions waiting on Paul or Russ. Do not invent generic priorities or reproduce the full queue.

### Review Execution

#### Intake and Reconciliation

- [ ] Process every [[00 Dashboard/Inbox|Inbox]] item: file ready evidence, preserve source links, and leave only unresolved items with an owner/status/next action.
- [ ] Compare meaningful repository changes with the previous report and reconcile conflicting or stale state.
- [ ] Reconcile every active [[00 Dashboard/Biweekly Review Queue|queue]] item, including status, owner, dependency, priority, next action, and next review.
- [ ] Reconcile past-due events and review the next two to four weeks of milestones and dependencies.

#### People and Leader Pipeline

- [ ] Review available `Lead a Group` submissions and acknowledgment/follow-up evidence in the approved system.

- [ ] Review training/preparation, assignment, appointment, commissioning, and matters requiring Russ.
- [ ] Route pastoral, safeguarding, screening, and sensitive assessment matters through the restricted process without copying details here.

#### Operations and Readiness

- [ ] Review Planning Center workflows, participant paths, permissions, and testing evidence.

#### Governance

- [ ] Confirm approved decisions and their evidence appear in the [[00 Dashboard/Decision Log|Decision Log]] and [[08 Archive/Decisions/Decision History|Decision History]].
- [ ] Reconcile material metadata in the [[00 Dashboard/Governance Index|Governance Index]] and remove resolved questions from [[00 Dashboard/Open Decisions|Open Decisions]].

- [ ] Verify approved decisions propagated downstream, superseded assumptions are marked, and unresolved decisions have an owner and next review when known.

#### System Write-Back

- [ ] Update affected canonical ministry, operations, calendar, rhythm, and decision pages.
- [ ] Update queue statuses and carry forward every unresolved item.
- [ ] Refresh the Groups Ministry and Launch Readiness dashboards only where current state changed.
- [ ] Historicize completed events, remove stale future tense, verify links/owners/next actions, and set the next review date.

### Risks / Attention

Record only applicable, non-sensitive categories and omit empty ones from the finished report:

- **Launch readiness**
- **Leader capacity**
- **Communication / participant path**
- **Safeguarding / pastoral escalation**
- **Ownership ambiguity**
- **Overdue follow-up**
- **Russ attention**

Reference the restricted system for sensitive matters; do not reproduce them.

### Priority Actions

Include three to eight highest-leverage actions derived from the queue:

| Priority | Action | Owner | Due / review by | Queue source | Status |
|---:|---|---|---|---|---|

Every row must reference an existing queue item or an item added during the review. The report must not create report-only tasks.

### Review Completion

- [ ] All required review domains were inspected or explicitly marked not applicable.
- [ ] Inbox and active queue were reconciled.
- [ ] Past events were reconciled or routed to human input.
- [ ] Canonical write-back, dashboard refresh, and Paul/Russ questions were completed where needed.
- [ ] Carry-forward items and the next review were recorded.

Then record review-execution status, human-review status, completed by, date completed, items carried forward, and next review. Ministry blockers may remain open after execution is complete.

## Review Report Structure

Each report uses the execution layer in this template, followed by concise findings: Executive Status; What Changed; Launch Readiness Movement; Ministry Area Review; Events and Decisions; Queue; Risks / Attention; Paul and Russ Questions; Priority Actions; Review Completion; and Next Review. Omit empty finding subsections rather than manufacturing activity.
