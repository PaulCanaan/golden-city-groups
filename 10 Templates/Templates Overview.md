## Boundaries and Shared Format

Template documents state **status, purpose, owner/audience, trigger, variables or inputs, reusable body, before-use checks, and workflow/source connections**. In a message library, the stage-specific metadata stays beside each message. Use the closest existing template rather than adding a second version elsewhere.

No prayer-request, incident, safeguarding, or confidential-assessment record is approved by this reorganization. [[06 Resources/Group Life/Prayer Requests|Prayer Requests]] and the policy/decision pages retain those open questions. Historical filled forms and message examples remain in their dated sources.
