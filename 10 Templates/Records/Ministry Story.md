# Ministry Story

## Document Status

**Draft non-identifying planning template; not consent or publication approval.**

## Purpose and Use

Plan a truthful account of formation without names or sensitive details. Collection and publication remain subject to the story-stewardship boundaries.

- **Owner:** Approved ministry leader; specific review/publication ownership remains to be confirmed.
- **Trigger:** A verified formation signal may be described without identifying participants.
- **Variables / inputs:** Environment, formation signal, non-identifying summary, follow-up owner, and actual publication status.

## Minimum Non-Identifying Draft

- **Environment:** City Group / Bible Club / Class / Collective
- **Formation signal:** Belonging / Beholding / Becoming
- **Brief summary:** No names or sensitive details
- **Follow-up owner:** Approved ministry leader
- **Publication status:** Internal draft / approved channel / not for publication

This template is for planning only and should not be used to collect identifiable participant information before policy approval.
