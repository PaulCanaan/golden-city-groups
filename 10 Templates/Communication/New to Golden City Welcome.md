## Document Status

**Draft template, rewritten October 5, 2026; not yet cleared to send.** A welcome for people who are new to Golden City Church, sent after they fill out the Connection Card following their first visit (Paul, October 5, 2026). It introduces who Golden City is and how to get involved: Next Steps, Groups, Serve Teams, City Kids, baptism, and the daily Bible reading plan. It comes in two forms: an email and a shorter text message. The earlier version, a pre-visit "what to expect" message, is replaced; it remains in version history.

Content comes from the church website, read October 5, 2026: the [home page](https://www.goldencity.church/), [About Us](https://www.goldencity.church/about), [Next Steps](https://www.goldencity.church/nextsteps), [Serve Team](https://www.goldencity.church/serveteam), [City Kids](https://www.goldencity.church/kids), [Baptism](https://www.goldencity.church/baptism), [Daily Bible](https://www.goldencity.church/bible), [You Said Yes](https://www.goldencity.church/yes), and [Give](https://www.goldencity.church/give).

This is **church-wide** communication, not a Groups Ministry procedure. The Connection Card is a church-wide form under Russ's participant-system ownership (DEC-032, DEC-057), and the repository records no owner or sender for newcomer follow-up. Paul drafted this template for the church; before it is used, Russ confirms:

1. **Who sends it.** The sender's name and role are variables. Do not send it in Russ's name unless he has reviewed and personalized it.
2. **The Connection Card's consent and routing.** In particular, whether the person agreed to be contacted by email and by text (BWR-009).

## Purpose and Ownership

Thank someone for their first visit and help them take a next step into the life of the church: knowing who Golden City is, the Next Steps classes, a group, a serve team, and what the church offers their family. **Owner:** to be confirmed by Russ. **Audience:** people who visited for the first time and filled out a Connection Card, whether they have followed Jesus for years or are just beginning to explore faith.

## Boundaries

- Use only what the website publishes. Recheck it before sending; class days, teams, and links can change.
- Offer, don't press. Point to Next Steps as the natural first step and let the rest be options. Leave giving out of a first welcome; generosity is introduced through the values and the Discover class.
- Do not invite a newcomer to lead a group. The serve page lists Small Group Leader, but leading follows its own conversation and discernment path (DEC-027, DEC-042).
- Do not name group schedules or the *Planted.* day and time. The website's Events page still lists Planted on Tuesdays, which no longer matches DEC-066; point to `goldencity.church/groups` instead.
- Do not ask for prayer requests or personal circumstances by email or text. Care conversations happen in person or through the church's care path (DEC-028).
- If the card also shows interest in Groups, Paul's [[10 Templates/Communication/Participant Welcome and Groups Introduction|Groups welcome]] follows separately. Coordinate so the person does not receive two overlapping emails on the same day.
- Record only aggregate, non-sensitive follow-up state. Names, contact details, and card contents stay in the church-approved restricted system.

## Email — Welcome to Golden City

- **Stage:** First visit; Connection Card received
- **Channel:** Email
- **Purpose:** Thank the person, introduce who Golden City is, and show the ways to get involved.
- **Trigger:** A first-time guest fills out the Connection Card after their first visit.
- **Owner:** To be confirmed
- **Variables:** `[First Name]`, `[Sender Name]`, `[Sender Role]`
- **Status:** Draft; blocked pending the Document Status confirmations

**Subject:** Welcome to Golden City — here's how to get connected

Hi [First Name],

Thank you for worshiping with us and for filling out a Connection Card. We're so glad you're here! Whether you've followed Jesus for years or are just beginning to explore faith, there's a place for you at Golden City.

**Who we are.** Golden City is a life-giving, non-denominational church in Broomfield. Our vision is to see our city fully following Jesus: a church committed to community, wholehearted in worship, and purposeful in prayer, a beacon of hope to the city. We exist to equip you to **forge your faith, find freedom in Christ, and follow Him fully.** And a few things we hold dear: Jesus is our pursuit, Scripture is our authority, the Holy Spirit is our power, people are our heart, service is our privilege, generosity is our lifestyle, and flourishing is our goal.

Here are a few ways to take your next step.

**Start with Next Steps.** Two short classes, right after the Sunday service, help you find your place here. *Discover* (first Sundays) introduces who we are, what we believe, and how we're led. *Connect* (second Sundays) helps you see how God has uniquely made you and connects you to a serve team, a group, and other areas of Golden City. You can register at [goldencity.church/nextsteps](https://www.goldencity.church/nextsteps).

**Find your people.** We like to say we're not a church that has groups; we're a church made up of groups. City Groups gather in homes and local spaces to share meals, pray, and care for one another. Our Men's and Women's Collectives are for walking alongside other men or women in faith, and our Bible Club is a guided study of Scripture together. You can explore this semester's groups at [goldencity.church/groups](https://www.goldencity.church/groups).

**Serve with us.** Service is our privilege, and there's a place for your gifts. Our Sunday Team welcomes people as greeters, parking team, ushers, prayer team, and set up and tear down. Our Kids Team disciples our kids and teaches them about Jesus. There are also Worship, Production, and Set Up teams. See the teams and sign up at [goldencity.church/serveteam](https://www.goldencity.church/serveteam).

**For your kids.** City Kids is a safe, engaging place for kids from 6 weeks through 5th grade, every Sunday at 10 AM, where children discover who God is, build real friendships, and grow in their faith. You can [register your family](https://goldencitychurchco.churchcenter.com/people/forms/1287032) ahead of time.

**A few more steps:**

- **Read the Bible with us.** Our daily reading plan takes you through the whole Bible in a year, a few short readings a day: [goldencity.church/bible](https://www.goldencity.church/bible).
- **Get baptized.** Ready to go public with your faith? We'd love to celebrate with you: [goldencity.church/baptism](https://www.goldencity.church/baptism).
- **New to following Jesus?** Start here: [goldencity.church/yes](https://www.goldencity.church/yes).

If you have any questions, or you're not sure where to start, just reply to this email. We'd love to help you find your place.

See you Sunday,

[Sender Name]<br>
[Sender Role]<br>
Golden City Church

*Optional:* delete the **For your kids** paragraph when the card shows no children.
