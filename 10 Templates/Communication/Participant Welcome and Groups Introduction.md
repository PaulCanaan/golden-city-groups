## Purpose and Ownership

Welcome someone who expressed interest on the church-wide Connection Card, introduce Groups Ministry, and give one truthful next step: browse the open groups and request to join, or reply for help finding a fit. **Owner:** Groups Director; Paul is the sender. **Audience:** people interested in participating or leading; this message does not place or appoint them. DEC-057 names email as the channel for welcome messages; use the text form as a short personal follow-up, not in place of the email.

## Boundaries

- The 16 Launch Sunday responses expressed interest in **participating**. Participation interest is not leader interest, appointment, or readiness. Do not send a Group Leader Conversation invitation on the strength of a Connection Card alone.
- Name groups only as they appear on the live directory. Do not repeat schedules, start dates, or areas in the message; they change (DEC-066), and the directory stays current. Never send an exact meeting location; those go privately to people who join.
- Requesting to join is a **request**. Do not promise a place, a reply time, or that a particular group has room.
- Record only aggregate, non-sensitive pipeline state. Names, contact details, card contents, and pastoral notes stay in the church-approved restricted system.

## Email — Groups Welcome and Introduction

- **Stage:** Participant interest received (Connection Card)
- **Channel:** Email
- **Purpose:** Thank the person, introduce what Groups are and why they exist, and point them to the open groups.
- **Trigger:** A person indicates on a Connection Card that they are interested in Groups, whether in participating or leading (DEC-057).
- **Owner:** Groups Director
- **Variables:** `[First Name]`
- **Status:** Draft; blocked pending the Document Status confirmations

**Subject:** Welcome to Golden City Groups

Hi [First Name],

Thank you for filling out a Connection Card and letting us know you're interested in Groups. I'm Paul, and I serve as the Ministry Director for Groups and Classes here at Golden City Church. I'm so glad you reached out, and I'd love to help you find your people.

Groups exist because following Jesus was never meant to be a solitary thing. Sunday morning is where we gather as a church; groups are the smaller rooms where we actually get known — where we open Scripture together, pray for one another, and carry each other's real lives through the week. Our hope is simple: that you would find a place to belong, that together we would keep our eyes on Jesus, and that over time the Spirit would form us into people who look like him.

Our fall groups have launched and are beginning to meet this month, and you're warmly invited to join one. There are three ways to connect:

- **City Groups** gather in homes and local spaces across our area to share meals, pray, and care for one another. Each has its own rhythm, and this fall they include a group for young adults and a Run Club.
- **Collectives** are for walking alongside other men or women. Our Men's Collective and Women's Collective each open with a short devotional that leads into small-group discussion, prayer, and encouragement.
- **Bible Club** is a guided study of a book or section of the Bible. This fall, *Planted.* Bible Study is a twelve-week journey through the whole story of Scripture, especially welcoming for anyone exploring Christianity or new to faith.

**How to join.** You can meet the leaders and see when and where each group meets here: [goldencity.church/groups/fall2026](https://www.goldencity.church/groups/fall2026). Choose a group and select **Join Group**, and its Church Center page will walk you through requesting to join. Exact meeting locations are shared privately once you're connected.

**Not sure which one fits?** Just reply to this email and tell me a little about yourself: what part of town you're in, what season of life you're in, and what you're hoping to find in a group. I'd be glad to help you find a good fit.

You don't need to have it all figured out to belong here. Come as you are, and let's see what God does.

Grateful you're here,

Paul<br>
Groups Ministry<br>
Golden City Church

## Optional Paragraphs

### Late Follow-Up

Add after the first paragraph of the email when the person filled out their card some time ago, such as the Launch Sunday responses in BWR-021. In the text, add *"Sorry for the slow follow-up!"* after the first sentence.

> I'm sorry this note is reaching you later than I'd hoped, and thank you for your patience.

### Leadership Curiosity

Include only when the person's Connection Card actually indicated interest in leading or hosting, or when they raise it themselves. Do not add it to a general participation reply, and do not treat a reply to it as entry into the workflow.

> You also mentioned being curious about leading or hosting a group. I'd love to talk about that. It starts with filling out the [Lead a Group form](https://goldencitychurchco.churchcenter.com/people/forms/1277943) and then grabbing coffee so I can hear your story and we can discern the right next step together — nothing decided in advance, just a conversation.

Anyone who responds enters at [[10 Templates/Communication/Group Leader Communication Library#Step 0 — Interest Submitted|Step 0 — Interest Submitted]] through the approved `Lead a Group` form, not through this email.
