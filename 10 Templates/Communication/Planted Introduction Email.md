## Email — Planted Introduction and Request Received

- **Stage:** Participant join request received (Church Center)
- **Channel:** Email
- **Purpose:** Confirm the request, introduce what *Planted* is and who it is for, and name the next step and when it will come.
- **Trigger:** A person requests to join *Planted* for the Fall 2026 semester.
- **Owner:** Groups Director
- **Variables:** `[First Name]`, `[Time]`, `[Location]`, `[What to Bring / Preparation]`, `[Response Window]`
- **Status:** Draft; blocked until the venue, calendar, capacity, and participant path are confirmed and tested

**Subject:** Planted — your request, and what this study is

Hi [First Name],

Thank you for requesting to join *Planted* this fall. I'm Paul, the Ministry Director for Groups and Classes at Golden City Church, and I'll be leading this study. I'm glad you want to be in the room.

Here's what *Planted* is.

*Planted* is a seeker- and new-believer-friendly journey through the biblical story and the life of following Jesus. Moving from creation to new creation, it invites you to see Scripture as one coherent story centered on Christ — and to locate your own life within that story.

It's biblically outlined, Christ-centered, and formation-oriented. Over five chapters and five recurring frameworks, it weaves together Scripture, biblical theology, experiential learning, conversation, prayer, worship, and community. The aim isn't only that you would understand the Christian faith, but that you would come to inhabit it.

The name is the picture. Like a seed planted in the ground, faith is meant to take root, rise into new life, grow over time, bear fruit, and plant again.

**Who it's for.** If you're exploring Christianity and not sure what you believe, you are genuinely welcome — bring your questions, and expect them to be taken seriously. If you're new to faith and want to understand the Bible as a whole rather than in scattered pieces, this is built for you. And if you've walked with Jesus for years, you'll find this is a good place to see the whole story again and to help others find their way into it.

You don't need any background in the Bible. You don't need to have answers ready. Come as you are.

**What I can tell you now.** *Planted* runs twelve weeks, beginning Tuesday evening, October 6, 2026. It's discussion-based, so plan on talking as much as listening.

**What's next.** Your request has been received. I'm finalizing the meeting location and the full session calendar now, and I'll follow you up with those confirmed details and everything you'll need for the first night [Response Window]. Your spot isn't confirmed until you hear back from me — I'll let you know either way.

In the meantime, if you have a question about the study or want to tell me a bit about where you're coming from, just reply to this email. I'd love to hear from you before we start.

Grateful you're taking this step,

Paul<br>
Groups Ministry<br>
Golden City Church
