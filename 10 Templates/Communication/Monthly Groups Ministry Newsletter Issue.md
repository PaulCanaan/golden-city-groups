## Launch Issue Checklist

Use these points at the beginning of each group launch. Keep each introduction short and link leaders to the current materials.

- [ ] Connect Groups & Classes to Golden City Church's mission, vision, core values, and theological foundations. Use the [[01 Governance/Ministry Context#Church Alignment|approved GCC language]] and the [[01 Governance/Theological Framework|ministry's theological framework]].
- [ ] Introduce [[02 Group Philosophy/Formation Framework/Belonging, Beholding, Becoming|Belonging, Beholding, and Becoming]] and give an example of how it shapes a gathering through community, prayer, and Scripture.
- [ ] Explain who to contact for ministry support and how to communicate with members. Link the [[07 Operations/Workflows/Ministry Communication Workflow#Channels|confirmed channels]] and provide one concrete next step.
- [ ] Show leaders where to find the [Groups Resources Library](https://resources.goldencity.church/) and the guide for their role. Invite leaders to share questions and practical lessons so the ministry can grow together in the church's direction.
- [ ] Confirm launch dates and any action a new or returning leader needs to take.
