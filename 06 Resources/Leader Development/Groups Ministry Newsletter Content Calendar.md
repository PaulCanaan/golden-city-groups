# Groups Ministry Newsletter Content Calendar

**Send rhythm.** First Saturday of each month at **8:00 a.m. Mountain Time** (`America/Denver`), by Planning Center email to current group leaders. Dates below are planning slots. Paul confirms each issue and its audience before delivery.

## Rhythm for Every Issue

- **At Golden City Church.** Start with staff-meeting and internal updates Paul shares. Use the [[07 Operations/Workflows/Monthly Groups Ministry Newsletter Production#Church Events Researcher|Church Events Researcher]] to verify public details and pick up relevant announcements from GCC's website and official social channels.
- **In Groups & Classes.** Share confirmed ministry news, launch and Collaboration dates, resource releases, and any action leaders need to take. Use the [[00 Dashboard/Calendar|Groups calendar]] and [[07 Operations/Annual Rhythm/Annual Calendar|annual rhythm]].
- **Leader equipping.** Include one short article with a Scripture connection and a practical next step. During Advent and Lent, a short devotional with a prayer or discussion prompt can fill this slot. Use the Christian calendar to shape reflection and GCC's calendar to confirm local observances.

## Launch Issues

Use the monthly issue nearest each Group Launch. Aim for the first-Saturday issue before the launch when possible. The February 6 issue is the planned orientation for the February 7 Spring Group Launch. Use the [[10 Templates/Communication/Monthly Groups Ministry Newsletter Issue#Launch Issue Checklist|launch issue checklist]] for the mission, vision, values, formation framework, leader communication, and resource access. Refresh these in the fall launch issue and use the checklist for any additional launch that is confirmed.
