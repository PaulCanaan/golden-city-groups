# First Meetings Checklist

## Document Status

**Proposed launch tool.** Use only for a group that has approved leadership, location, schedule, safeguarding, participant-data practices, and launch-readiness approval.

## Ministry Purpose

Help a prepared leader begin with warmth, clarity, prayer, Scripture, and dependable follow-through rather than improvising important responsibilities.

## Before the First Meeting

- [ ] Pray for each known participant and for the Spirit's wisdom.
- [ ] Confirm the approved date, start and end time, location, access instructions, and capacity.
- [ ] Confirm the Planning Center event, roster, reminders, and approved communication channel.
- [ ] Review the approved content for this environment and identify the central Scripture, purpose, and response.
- [ ] Prepare a clean, accessible, and welcoming environment.
- [ ] Plan a simple arrival experience so newcomers know where to go and whom to meet.
- [ ] Confirm any approved host, co-leader, childcare, minor, accessibility, or safety arrangements.
- [ ] Know the primary and backup pastoral escalation contacts.
- [ ] Send an accurate reminder with what participants need to know.

## During the Meeting

- [ ] Welcome people personally and make introductions without forcing disclosure.
- [ ] Explain the group's purpose, normal rhythm, beginning and ending time, communication channel, and confidentiality limits.
- [ ] Use a low-pressure opening question.
- [ ] Create room for participants to speak; facilitate rather than dominate.
- [ ] Keep the gathering centered on its approved environment purpose, Scripture, prayer, and faithful response.
- [ ] Pray with consent and without promising secrecy or outcomes.
- [ ] Redirect divisive, unsafe, or overly personal discussion appropriately.
- [ ] End on time and explain the next gathering or next step.

## After the Meeting

- [ ] Record attendance through the approved Planning Center procedure.
- [ ] Welcome or follow up with first-time participants through the approved channel.
- [ ] Correct inaccurate roster or event information.
- [ ] Move any pastoral, safeguarding, or crisis concern immediately to the approved escalation process.
- [ ] Ask what helped people connect and what needs adjustment.
- [ ] Check in with the host or co-leader and prepare for the next gathering.

## Formation Relationship

- **Belonging:** personal welcome, clarity, inclusion, and follow-up.
- **Beholding:** Scripture, prayer, and attention to Christ remain central.
- **Becoming:** participants respond faithfully and begin serving one another.

## Responsibility and Approval

The approved group leader owns this checklist with support from Paul or the assigned ministry leader. Completion does not replace pastoral approval or the launch checklist.
