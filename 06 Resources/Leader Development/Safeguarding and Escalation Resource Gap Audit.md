# Safeguarding and Escalation Resource Gap Audit

## Document Status

**Evidence-based gap audit for pastoral review; not policy, legal guidance, or readiness approval.** Reviewed August 10, 2026. This audit classifies repository evidence and identifies decisions and implementation evidence still required. It does not create reporting duties, response procedures, contacts, conduct rules, or pastoral-care protocols.

## Executive Finding

Golden City Church Groups Ministry has an approved **minimum boundary**, not a complete safeguarding and escalation system. DEC-027, DEC-028, and DEC-033 establish appointment, confidentiality, care, safeguarding, participant-data, prayer, and escalation limits. DEC-021 reserves safeguarding and participant-data policy, primary-leader appointment or removal, and exceptions to Russ. DEC-039 authorizes research and development, not policy.

The repository does not locate a church-wide safeguarding, child-protection, vulnerable-adult, mandatory-reporting, incident-response, emergency-action, conduct, privacy, retention, screening, or pastoral-care policy. It also does not name primary or backup escalation contacts or provide an approved incident/reporting path. Those are evidence gaps; they do not prove that no church policy exists.

Under current evidence:

- **City Groups are Blocked** from host/leader approval and public operation where homes, participants, care, or escalation are involved. No host, leader, home, safeguarding implementation, escalation path, or final readiness approval is recorded.
- **Planted is At Risk overall and Blocked on this safety gate** before participants begin. Paul and the curriculum are approved, but the location, participant path, support role, escalation contact, system settings, and final readiness remain open.
- **Collectives are applicable but not the immediate audited launch track.** Their operational owners are named; leaders, locations, escalation contacts, participant paths, and readiness dispositions remain open.
- **Minors, childcare, vulnerable people, transportation, and overnight activity are conditional.** Approved safeguards must be confirmed before any such activity begins; the repository does not establish that these features are part of the initial plan.

City Group recruitment conversations and non-sensitive administrative preparation may continue within DEC-026 and DEC-031. Hosts or leaders should not be approved, trained as operationally complete, given participant access, publicly listed, or placed with participants until the launch-critical minimum below is evidenced and final readiness is approved.

## Launch-Critical Minimum

The minimum is evidence of approved categories, not invented policy content.

### 1. Before City Group hosts are approved
