# Ministry Resources

## Document Status

**Central resource library with mixed-status materials.** This page is the single entry point for reusable Groups Ministry resources. Each linked document retains its own approval status. A resource does not become governing merely because it appears here.

## Purpose

Make the right resource easy to find without copying the same material into ministry branches, future Classes, Leadership, and Operations. Resources support ministry practice; role definitions remain under [[Leadership Overview]], operational instructions remain under [[Operation Overview]], and curriculum remains under [[Classes Overview]].

### Leader Resources and SOP Research Roadmap

DEC-039 directs the ministry to develop leader resources and standard operating procedures from research into healthy church practices. Research and placeholders may be developed for:

- safeguarding;
- pastoral care;
- mandatory reporting;
- leader expectations;
- volunteer care; and
- incident response.

For each topic, record the source, relevance to Golden City Church, proposed owner, required reviewer, approval status, and unresolved questions. Do not import another church's policy, state legal conclusions, or represent research notes as governing direction. The GCC Group Leader Agreement (DEC-077) now sets the group leader policies and names the escalation contacts; church-wide policy sources, the mandatory-reporting procedure, and final SOP approval remain open.

## Resource Stewardship

Paul currently owns resource development within confirmed church direction. DEC-027, DEC-028, and DEC-033 govern the approved minimum leadership, safeguarding, participant-data, and pastoral baselines, and DEC-077 approves the GCC Group Leader Agreement built on them. Russ or the appropriately delegated pastoral authority retains approval of their remaining policy implementation, disputed theological claims, and any resource explicitly marked as awaiting pastoral approval.

Before adding a new resource:

1. Confirm that a real ministry need exists.
2. Search this library for an existing resource to improve.
3. Give the new document one clear responsibility.
4. Link to governing material rather than copying it.
5. Mark its status, owner, audience, and approval needs.
6. Add it to this overview only when it contains useful content.

Deferred ideas, empty media lists, and future playbooks should not become navigation clutter. Create them when ministry need, usable content, capacity, and pastoral direction warrant development.
