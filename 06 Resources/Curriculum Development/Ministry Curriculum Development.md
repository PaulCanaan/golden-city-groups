## Purpose, People, and Formation

Turn scattered ideas and source material into coherent, Scripture-centered resources that people can use together: session presentations, participant handouts, course content, and table-leader notes. Begin with the biblical text and the people being served. Let the material invite people to behold Christ and respond in faith, prayer, community, and everyday obedience.

## Process Map

**Biblical Exegesis → Theological Synthesis → Formation Goals → Session Design → Resource Production**

Return to earlier stages whenever a source, interpretation, or review changes the direction. Work on the next needed session rather than building an entire course package at once.

| Stage | Action | Move forward when… |
|---|---|---|
| Biblical Exegesis | Read the passage in context; trace its argument, key words, literary structure, and historical questions. Link the Bible, Notion notes, and relevant source pages | Observations and interpretive claims are traceable to the text; unresolved questions remain visible |
| Theological Synthesis | Bring the exegesis into conversation with the biblical story, Christ, church theology, and properly attributed research | The central theological claim follows from the text, with source contributions and any disputed claim clearly identified |
| Formation Goals | Name how this truth invites Belonging, Beholding, and Becoming for these participants | Concrete invitations to trust, prayer, shared life, and faithful practice connect to the theological claim |
| Session Design | Select the central idea, teaching movements, discussion, experience, prayer, and response in the phase 02 outline; after Paul reviews direction, elaborate the phase 03 gathering master | The direction fits the branch and time, and Paul has confirmed the scope before editable production |
| Resource Production | Review content and applicable approvals, derive needed slides/handouts/leader notes, apply the resource style, inspect exports, and record current versions | Actual usable resources reflect the reviewed master and have correct layout, source references, and delivery information |

Frame the audience, ownership, scope, and outputs in the brief before starting. Review runs through every stage; learning after actual use feeds back into the relevant stage. These supporting actions do not add stages to the five-part development structure.

## Biblical Exegesis and Theological Synthesis

Use one card per idea. Keep the passage and its context visible while exploring connections to the biblical story, Christ, the life of the church, and faithful response. A compelling metaphor must serve the text rather than determine its meaning. Paul's biblical-theological interests can open fruitful questions; they do not establish church doctrine.

For each outside source, record its author/title, location or page, and what it supports. Distinguish a quotation from a paraphrase. Cite the Bible translation when reproducing Scripture. Keep unprovided texts, unverified quotations, and unavailable Notion material marked as missing.

Use [[06 Resources/Curriculum Development/01 Research & Brainstorm/README|the curriculum inbox]] for permitted research/brainstorm inputs, and link actual Notion knowledge rather than copying every source. The existing [[06 Resources/Resource Inbox/Resource Inbox|Resource Inbox]] remains the sermon-script drop folder for Weekly Recap production. This public repository must not receive confidential participant information, private teaching scripts, or unlicensed source reproductions. Notes should contain only material suitable for this repository; local-only files still follow its privacy boundaries.

## Formation Goals and Session Design

Use [[10 Templates/Records/Curriculum Session|a session master]] for the passage, central idea, contextual explanation, formation invitations, learning movements, questions, and needed output content. Each Collective has its own dated master and deliverables, informed by the shared Galatians research. Name its particular formation goals before choosing applications and questions; different goals require more than changing the title or date. Check that each major teaching point is supported by the passage or a named source. Preserve Paul's theological and pastoral voice without attributing unrecorded statements to leadership.

| Resource | Derive from the master | Usability check |
|---|---|---|
| Presentation slides | Session movements, key texts, brief claims, purposeful visuals, questions, and cues | Legible when projected; one main purpose per slide; facilitator detail belongs in notes |
| Participant handout | Passage references, essential explanation, a small set of questions, space to respond, and a faithful next step | Understandable without the deck; accessible to newcomers; enough room to write |
| Table-leader or facilitator notes | Session aim, passage context, question intent, follow-up prompts, likely misunderstandings, prayer/response cues, and ordinary care boundaries | A leader can guide participation without giving a second lecture or pressing for personal disclosure |
| Course content | The linked sequence of reviewed session masters | The course's biblical argument and formation arc remain coherent; missing sessions stay visibly missing |

Create only the outputs that the session actually needs. For Collectives, preserve the short devotional and table discussion shape. For Planted, preserve the discussion-based Bible Club model and its varied forms of learning. Activities need actual time, space, supplies, accessibility alternatives, and permission to opt out; a written activity is not evidence those needs are met.

Paul's current gathering-design envelope for Planted and the Collectives is **6:30–8:00 PM: 20 minutes community/check-in, approximately 60 minutes study/discussion, and 10 minutes closing prayer/worship/announcements**. Collectives use the [[10 Templates/Gatherings/Collective Gathering#Gathering Flow (6:30–8:00 PM)|two-block flow Russ approved]] (DEC-076). Planted's study hour retains its own approved learning movements; the two-block format is not automatically imposed on it. Revise the working design using actual execution and feedback.

## Review and Approval

The material owner reviews routine development within confirmed direction. There is no blanket requirement to send every draft or formatting adjustment to Russ.

**Russ's approval is required** for new or disputed doctrinal positions, additional prayer-ministry practices beyond the approved baseline, safeguarding or participant-data policy changes, material public ministry claims, significant budget/facility commitments, and exceptions to approved boundaries (DEC-021, DEC-033). Put the specific question and recommendation in the [[00 Dashboard/Staff Decision Brief|Staff Decision Brief]] when staff direction is needed. Record approved direction through the [[07 Operations/Workflows/Decision Recording Workflow|existing decision workflow]]. Do not create a DEC record for this workspace itself.

Record review evidence against the particular resource/version: who reviewed it, when, what was reviewed, and any remaining changes. Use these resource states in the project table: **Draft → In review → Ready to produce → Final resource → Revised**. They describe production only. A missing decision keeps the affected material in review; unrelated preparation can continue. Curriculum approval and offering readiness remain separately stated.

Planted's approved curriculum is the baseline. A missing local session file is a source/publishing gap, not proof that its curriculum is unapproved. Galatians approval and DEC-067 establish the study plan and Paul's resource-development role, not approval of every drafted devotional or table note.

## Produce, Verify, and File

1. Confirm the phase 03 master reflects corrections and Paul's actual editable-content review is recorded in the phase 02 project brief.
2. Create or update the needed editable deliverables. Name them consistently, for example `Planted S01 Slides` or `Mens Galatians G01A YYYY-MM-DD Handout` / `Womens Galatians G01A YYYY-MM-DD Table Leader Notes`. Replace date placeholders with the actual confirmed gathering date. Each Collective gets its own resource package; study IDs identify gatherings and Block 1/2 headings identify the two movements inside them.
3. Pass content through the [[06 Resources/Curriculum Development/Complexity Gate|Complexity Gate]] and keep question design consistent with the [[10 Templates/Gatherings/Weekly Recap|Weekly Recap]] (DEC-071). Apply [[06 Resources/Brand/Groups Resource Style|Groups Resource Style]] to printable resources. Use the approved source's title styling; for Planted, use *Planted.* Bible Study or the short form *Planted.* 2026 (DEC-070). Slides may adapt scale and spacing for projection.
4. Export the actual needed format: PDF for printing/sharing, an editable presentation for teaching, or both when required. Read the exported artifact itself, inspect every slide/page, and check clipping, font size, writing space, Scripture references, citations, image rights, and agreement with the master. Reopen the final file after a repair.
5. Present the actual export candidate for Paul's final review. Record his response/date/version in the project brief; after he confirms it, place the accepted edition in phase 04 and link its phase 03 editable source, audience/date, and current version. Keep one clearly identified current resource and preserve approved editions/historical evidence deliberately.
6. Share through the offering's established path only under task authorization or standing offering-specific direction, and record the actual destination/result. Library placement alone is not external publication. A completed export does not establish a location, appoint a leader, or authorize an offering.

When an AI agent is asked to produce a file, use the appropriate document, presentation, or PDF skill and its rendering checks. This system creates the development structure; it does not imply any slides, handouts, Notion pages, or PDFs have already been produced or published.

## Learn from Actual Use

After a session actually takes place, record only non-identifying observations: where participants could engage, where explanation or questions were unclear, whether the learning movement fit the available time, and what should change. Do not claim spiritual outcomes from attendance or a completed worksheet. Keep pastoral narratives and participant disclosures out of these notes.

Handle cross-project blockers and governance questions through the existing [[00 Dashboard/Biweekly Review Queue|review queue]] and [[07 Operations/Workflows/Biweekly Ministry Review|first/third-Monday review]]. This development process runs when a resource is needed and adds no parallel recurring review cadence.
