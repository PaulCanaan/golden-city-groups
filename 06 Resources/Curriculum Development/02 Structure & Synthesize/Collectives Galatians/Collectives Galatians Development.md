## Five-Stage Development

**Biblical Exegesis → Theological Synthesis → Formation Goals → Session Design → Resource Production**

1. Read the assigned Galatians chapter and select the passages for its two study gatherings; each gathering has two teaching/discussion blocks. Exact subdivisions remain a development task.
2. Develop the shared exegesis and theological synthesis primarily with Keller's *Galatians For You*. Use Peterson's *Traveling Light* secondarily, especially for its teaching on freedom; cite the actual contributions used.
3. Name separate Men's and Women's Belonging, Beholding, and Becoming goals, including their particular male/female discipleship focuses and pastoral rationale.
4. Create separate dated gathering masters with two 20+10 blocks each. Gather the base discussion questions from Keller and select/adapt them for each Collective's goals, with edition/page references.
5. Produce separate 2–3-page handouts and table-leader notes, plus slides where needed. Review each package against its own master, source references, goals, and actual date.

Keep the common biblical research linked so corrections to exegesis can be reconciled in both packages. Their formation goals, teaching examples, applications, selected questions, and delivery dates need independent attention. A shared course does not imply an identical final resource.
