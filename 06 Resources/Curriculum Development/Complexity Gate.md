# Complexity Gate

## Document Status

**Paul's design philosophy for Groups study resources; part of the resource design standard under [[08 Archive/Decisions/Decision History#DEC-071 — Groups Resource Design Standard|DEC-071]].** Paul set out the principle at the October 19, 2026 staff meeting with Russ ([[08 Archive/Decisions/2026-10-19 Staff Meeting Minutes#4 — The Complexity Gate (Paul's Design Principle)|minutes, item 4]]). The principle and the three zones are Paul's. The gate questions and branch notes below are a working draft built from the principle, for Paul to refine as he applies it. The first planned use is Paul's refactor of the Collectives Galatians study.

## The Question

How deep should a Bible study go in our groups and classes?

Most people don't find glory in complexity. Content that is too deep can overwhelm the very people a study is meant to form. Content that is too shallow leaves them where they started. The aim is not to show how much can be said about a passage, but to help people meet Christ in it and respond.

## Three Zones

Paul's pedagogical principle is to keep participants in neither the comfort zone nor the danger zone, but in the adventure zone.

| Zone | What it feels like | What happens |
|---|---|---|
| **Comfort zone** — too shallow | Familiar, safe, easily answered | Little is asked of anyone; people agree, and nothing moves |
| **Adventure zone** — the target | Exciting, a stretch, within reach | Curiosity and courage grow together; people discover something and want to take the next step |
| **Danger zone** — too deep | Overwhelming, technical, out of reach | People withdraw, defer to the "experts" in the room, or quietly decide Scripture isn't for them |

The adventure zone is where growth is challenged the most. It is not a compromise between shallow and deep. It is the place where a person is stretched just beyond where they stand, with enough footing to take the step.

## Why It Matters for Formation

Scripture itself teaches this way. Paul fed the Corinthians milk because they were not yet ready for solid food (1 Corinthians 3:2), and Hebrews longs for believers to grow toward solid food (Hebrews 5:12–14). Jesus spoke the word to the crowds "as they were able to hear" (Mark 4:33). Depth is measured by what forms people, not by how much is covered.

The gate also serves Belonging, Beholding, Becoming. People who feel lost do not belong in the conversation. People who are never stretched do not truly behold. Only a study that reaches people where they are, and invites them one step further, moves them toward becoming.

## The Gate

*Working draft for Paul's refinement.* Before a piece of content goes into a participant resource, ask:

1. **Does it serve the passage's main point?** If it is interesting but not essential, it waits.
2. **Can a participant engage with only this page and a Bible?** Background a participant needs belongs in a short sentence of context, not a lecture.
3. **Is it one step beyond where this group is?** Name the step. If it takes three steps to reach, it is the danger zone; if it takes none, it is the comfort zone.
4. **Does it invite a response?** Each movement should lead toward conversation, prayer, or faithful action, not only toward knowing more.
5. **Where does the extra depth go?** Rich material that doesn't pass the gate isn't wasted. It moves to leader or table-leader notes, an optional "go deeper" line, or a future Bible Club or Class where sustained study is the purpose.

## Across the Branches

The same gate applies everywhere, but each branch sets the starting point differently. These follow the length and depth targets in [[06 Resources/Brand/Groups Resource Style#Length and Depth by Ministry Branch|Groups Resource Style]].

- **City Groups:** life together comes first. The Weekly Recap lives near the edge of the comfort zone and stretches through its discussion questions.
- **Collectives:** shared encouragement with real study. The handout carries one clear stretch per study block; the deeper exegesis lives in the teaching and the table-leader notes.
- **Bible Clubs and future Classes:** sustained study is the purpose, so the adventure zone sits further in. The gate still guards against the danger zone.
