# Gentleness and Self-Control

**Weekly Recap · November 22, 2026** · *The Fruit of the Spirit* · Week 8

## Check In

1. What are you looking forward to this Thanksgiving week?
2. What's one thing from Sunday's message that has stuck with you?

## This Week's Message

Sunday's message closed *The Fruit of the Spirit* with gentleness and self-control, framed by a bridle. A few ounces of iron steer a horse of twelve hundred pounds, and the horse isn't weaker for it. That is strength under control. It started with Jesus, who describes His own heart as gentle and lowly (Matthew 11:29). In the garden His words knocked down an armed crowd, yet He healed the ear of a servant who came to arrest Him (Luke 22:51). Gentleness is strength under control toward others. We restore someone who has fallen with a gentle spirit (Galatians 6:1), the way David spared Saul in the cave, and a gentle answer turns away anger (Proverbs 15:1). Self-control is strength under control within us. Without it we are a city with broken walls (Proverbs 25:28), so we decide our no before the moment shows up, like a letter written and never sent. It is fruit of the Spirit, not of trying harder. Both grow as we keep in step with the Spirit (Galatians 5:25) and hand Jesus the reins.

## Read Together

Galatians 5:22–26; 2 Timothy 2:24–25

## Discuss

1. What stands out to you in Galatians 5:22–26 and 2 Timothy 2:24–25?
2. Jesus had all power in the garden, and He still chose to heal. What does that tell you about His gentle and lowly heart?
3. 2 Timothy 2:24 says the Lord's servant must be gentle to everyone. Who's hardest for you to be gentle with right now?
4. What tends to get in the way when you try to answer gently instead of reacting?

## Pray

1. Thank Jesus for His gentle and lowly heart, and that He came to heal, not to trample.
2. Ask the Spirit to grow gentleness in us toward the people around our tables this week.
3. Ask Him for self-control, to decide our no before the moment shows up and bring it to Him first.
4. Pray that we keep in step with the Spirit, hand Jesus the reins, and step back on when we slip.
