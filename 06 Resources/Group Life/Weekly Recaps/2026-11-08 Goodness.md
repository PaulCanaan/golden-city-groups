# Goodness

**Weekly Recap · November 8, 2026** · *The Fruit of the Spirit* · Week 6

## Check In

1. What's something you had to try for yourself before you believed it was good?
2. Where did you see or receive goodness this week?

## This Week's Message

Sunday's message continued *The Fruit of the Spirit* with goodness. If kindness is the hands, goodness is the heart those hands come from. Fruit reveals what we're rooted in, and God isn't after good-looking; He's after good all the way through (Matthew 7:17–18). First, only God is truly good (Mark 10:18). Goodness isn't just something He does; it's who He is, and it starts not with trying harder but with tasting and seeing that He is good (Psalm 34:8). Second, goodness starts at the root. Whatever is in the heart comes out (Luke 6:45), and God doesn't polish us; He promises a new heart (Ezekiel 36:26). Third, goodness has a backbone. Nice avoids the hard thing; good does it anyway, because nice keeps the peace, but good makes it right (Romans 12:9, 21). Fourth, don't get tired of doing good (Galatians 6:9). At the center is Jesus, the only truly good One, who took everything rotten in us so we could be made new from the root (2 Corinthians 5:21). So keep going; doing good won't be wasted.

2. What does it tell you about God that He promises to do the changing Himself?

## Pray

1. Thank God that He is good all the way through.
2. Ask Him to change what's in us at the root, not just how we look.
3. Ask for courage to do the good thing when being nice would be easier.
4. Pray for one another, especially for anyone who is tired of doing good.
