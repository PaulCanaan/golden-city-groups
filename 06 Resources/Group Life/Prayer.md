# Prayer in Groups

## Document Status

**Confirmed minimum baseline under DEC-033; detailed prayer-ministry practices still require explicit pastoral approval and training.**

## Ministry Purpose

Prayer directs the group toward Christ, expresses dependence on the Holy Spirit, and enables members to bear one another's burdens within faithful pastoral boundaries.

## Leader Practice

- Pray for wisdom, humility, and each participant before the gathering.
- Invite prayer as part of the group's response to Scripture and shared life.
- Explain that people may participate without being required to pray aloud or disclose a need.
- Ask consent before praying personally for someone or sharing a request beyond the immediate setting.
- Keep prayers Christ-centered, Scripture-shaped, honest, and understandable.
- Encourage participants to pray for one another without turning prayer into advice, diagnosis, or correction.
- Follow up relationally where appropriate through the approved communication channel.

## Confidentiality and Escalation

- Do not promise absolute confidentiality.
- Do not place identifiable prayer requests in this repository, general event notes, or group chat unless the church has explicitly approved that practice and the person has consented.
- Imminent danger, suspected abuse, threats of harm, and safeguarding concerns move immediately to the approved reporting and escalation process.
- Leaders do not present themselves as sole crisis responders, counselors, or authorities over medical or mental-health decisions.

## Formation Relationship

- **Belonging:** members carry burdens and celebrate God's faithfulness together.
- **Beholding:** prayer directs attention, dependence, worship, and hope toward Christ.
- **Becoming:** participants grow in compassion, faith, repentance, endurance, and service.

## Responsibility and Approval

The approved leader facilitates prayer within the church's boundaries. Practices related to spiritual gifts, laying on of hands, extended ministry prayer, deliverance, healing claims, or prayer involving minors require explicit pastoral direction and training.
