# Hospitality

## Document Status

**Proposed leader resource.** Specific accessibility, home-meeting, childcare, minor, and facility requirements remain subject to pastoral and safeguarding approval.

## Ministry Purpose

Hospitality helps people move from attendance toward genuine participation in the life of Christ's people. It is not entertainment or presentation; it is attentive welcome, clarity, generosity, and room for others.

## Before People Arrive

- Pray for the people expected and for those who may be new.
- Confirm the approved time, location, access instructions, seating, and communication.
- Remove avoidable barriers related to cleanliness, lighting, noise, temperature, accessibility, pets, parking, or confusing entry instructions.
- Prepare the host and leader to welcome people by name and notice anyone arriving alone.
- Keep food simple and optional; do not create an expectation leaders or hosts cannot sustain.

## During the Gathering

- Introduce people naturally without requiring personal disclosure.
- Explain the group's purpose, rhythm, ending time, communication channel, and confidentiality limits.
- Use a low-pressure opening question and make space for quieter participants.
- Invite participation without pressuring anyone to speak, pray aloud, or disclose sensitive information.
- Facilitate more than you speak. Redirect a person who repeatedly dominates and draw others in gently.
- Avoid unexplained church language, insider references, or assumptions about spiritual maturity.
- End on time and make the next step clear.

## After the Gathering

- Follow up with first-time participants through the approved channel.
- Notice practical needs without making promises beyond the group's authority or capacity.
- Ask the host or co-leader what helped people participate and what should change next time.

## Formation Relationship

- **Belonging:** people are welcomed, known, included, and treated with dignity.
- **Beholding:** the environment makes room to attend to Christ together.
- **Becoming:** participants begin offering welcome, care, service, and generosity to others.

## Responsibility and Approval

The approved group leader is responsible for the participant experience; the host is responsible for the agreed location duties. Their exact division of responsibility remains pending. Safeguarding and accessibility requirements override convenience.
