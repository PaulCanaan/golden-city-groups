# Weekly Recaps

## Document Status

**Approved optional City Group resource with named owners and a working delivery rhythm.** Under DEC-055, Russ and Paul prepare the Weekly Recap from each weekend message, with check-in questions and discussion questions. Under the October 7, 2026 clarification of DEC-055, Russ sends the sermon script on Sunday afternoon, and Paul approves and posts the recap as a PDF in the All Group Leaders Church Center chat on Sunday evening ([[07 Operations/Workflows/Weekly Recap Production|Weekly Recap Production]]). Each recap is kept in this repository as a published resource. Russ affirmed its format and content on October 19, 2026, and it is now the model for other Groups resources (DEC-071); a public Groups resources page is planned to carry it (DEC-072). City Group leaders are not required to use it (DEC-052); it does not define a City Group and is not an administrative leader report.

## Purpose

When a group chooses to use it, help people carry the weekend message into shared reflection, prayer, pastoral connection, and faithful response while preserving the City Group purpose of life together.

## Suggested Shape

Each recap opens with check-in questions so people can share how their week has actually gone, gives a short summary for anyone who missed the message, then moves into discussion questions that go deeper into the weekend message, and closes with a prayer prompt. [[10 Templates/Gatherings/Weekly Recap|Weekly Recap]] holds the structure. It is optional, supports conversation rather than testing recall, and remains distinct from an administrative leader report.

## Leader Use

- Read the recap before the gathering and choose the questions most useful for the group.
- Let conversation breathe; completing every question is not the goal.
- Draw quieter people in without pressuring them.
- Keep the discussion connected to Scripture and the weekend message.
- Name a possible response without forcing public disclosure.
- Move pastoral or safeguarding concerns into the approved care process.

Do not record participant names, identifiable prayer requests, counseling notes, or sensitive pastoral information in the recap or this repository.
