# Conversation Starters

## Document Status

**Optional leader resource.** These prompts support welcome and relationship; they are not a required meeting script.

## Facilitation Guidance

- Choose one question suited to the group's trust and maturity.
- Answer briefly as the leader so the invitation feels mutual.
- Let people pass without explanation.
- Avoid questions that solicit trauma, confession, finances, politics, medical details, or private family information.
- Move from light conversation toward meaningful relationship naturally rather than forcing depth.

## Low-Pressure Prompts

- What is something you have enjoyed recently?
- What brought you to Golden City Church or this group?
- What is one place in the area you enjoy visiting?
- What is something you are looking forward to this season?
- What is a skill, hobby, or interest you would enjoy sharing with others?
- What helps you feel welcomed when you enter a new group?

## Scripture and Sermon Prompts

- What stayed with you from Sunday's message?
- What question did the biblical text raise for you?
- Where did you see hope, challenge, or grace in the passage?
- What might faithful response look like this week?
