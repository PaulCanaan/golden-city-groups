# Discipleship

## Document Status

**Proposed leader resource governed by the confirmed formation framework.** Specific public pathways, membership relationships, baptism practices, and leadership-development standards remain subject to pastoral approval.

## Ministry Purpose

Help people forge their faith, find freedom in Christ, and follow Jesus fully through Scripture, prayer, community, obedience, service, generosity, and gospel witness. Leaders accompany people relationally; they do not move them through an automatic program ladder.

## Relational Discernment

A next faithful step is best discovered through prayer, Scripture, conversation, and knowledge of the person rather than inferred from attendance data. Leaders may ask:

- What has God been teaching or revealing to you?
- Where are you experiencing grace, struggle, conviction, or hope?
- What practice or act of obedience would help you respond faithfully?
- Who could pray with, encourage, or serve alongside you?
- How can the group support you without taking over responsibility that belongs to you or pastoral leadership?

## Belonging, Beholding, Becoming

### Belonging

- receive welcome and offer welcome to others
- build trustworthy relationships and participate in shared care
- reconnect when absence, conflict, or isolation begins to weaken community

### Beholding

- attend to Christ through Scripture, prayer, worship, and the life of the church
- ask honest questions and grow in biblical understanding
- practice repentance, faith, gratitude, and dependence on the Holy Spirit

### Becoming

- embody Christlike character in everyday relationships and work
- serve the church and neighbor with gifts, time, and generosity
- practice reconciliation, hospitality, witness, and faithful responsibility
- help form others without assuming that maturity requires a title

These dimensions reinforce one another and should not be presented as a rigid sequence.

## Leadership Development Boundary

Leaders may notice and encourage service, teachability, character, gifts, and relational care. They may offer bounded opportunities such as welcoming, reading Scripture, facilitating an approved question, or serving practically. They must not promise apprenticeship, training admission, appointment, or a future group.

For fall 2026, initial City Group leaders come from the Launch Team through the confirmed Team Night, discernment, training, assignment, and commissioning process. Broader development may emerge later.
