# Women's Collective Leader Guide

## Document Status

**Draft role companion under the approved minimum standard; individual leadership and appointment remain open.** This page applies shared GCC resources to Women's Collective without creating a separate leadership policy.

## Leader Responsibility

An appointed leader would steward the approved Collective purpose, gathering plan, participant care, communication, and follow-through under pastoral oversight. Interest, Launch Team participation, training, or Planning Center access does not constitute appointment.

## Before Launch

- complete the approved discernment and role-specific preparation
- confirm the schedule, location, capacity, content, safeguarding plan, and participant path
- understand confidentiality limits and primary and backup escalation contacts
- verify the Church Center page, notifications, and approved communication channel
- use the [[First Meetings Checklist]] before the first gathering

## Gathering Practice

- cultivate welcome, participation, Scripture, prayer, and faithful response
- facilitate rather than dominate
- keep the gathering aligned with the gender-specific Collective purpose
- avoid unapproved curriculum, policy, theological claims, fundraising, or commitments
- move care, conflict, safeguarding, and crisis concerns to the approved process

## After the Gathering

- complete approved attendance and follow-up
- correct inaccurate participant or event information
- escalate urgent concerns immediately
- receive support through the approved [[Leader Support Check-In]] rhythm

## Adaptation Note

This guide applies original GCC resources developed after review of Church of the Highlands materials. See [[Highlands Resource Adaptation]]. It does not import Highlands' hierarchy, honor code, or policies.

## Approval Still Required

DEC-027–029 resolve the minimum appointment and care standards and assign operational ownership. PRO-027 and PRO-030 must still resolve individual leaders, escalation contacts, readiness, and participant-system details.
