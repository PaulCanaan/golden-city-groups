# Women's Collective Purpose

## Document Status

**Confirmed environment classification; working offering description.** Women's Collective is an expression of the approved Collectives environment. Its launch readiness and detailed gathering plan remain open.

## Purpose

Provide a gender-specific ministry environment in which women can build Christ-centered community, receive pastoral connection, engage Scripture and prayer, and respond faithfully as disciples of Jesus.

Women's Collective is not a subtype of City Groups, Bible Clubs, or Classes. It shares the Belonging, Beholding, and Becoming framework while serving a distinct ministry purpose.

## Current Boundaries

- The published fall rhythm and location are communication baselines, not proof of readiness.
- Leadership, capacity, curriculum, safeguarding, participant path, location verification, and final approval remain unresolved.
- Percentage-based definitions are superseded and should not guide or describe the offering.
