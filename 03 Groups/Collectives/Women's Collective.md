## Ministry Emphasis

Women's Collective is a confirmed expression of the Collectives branch, whose purpose is shared encouragement. Its gatherings should cultivate community, prayer, pastoral connection, biblical formation, and faithful response among women.

The approved long-term direction is for Women's Collective to mature into a broader Women's Ministry. This does not activate additional programs or establish their leaders, rhythms, or readiness.

The co-leads are Miranda Daly and Niki Pike. Niki stepped in on October 11, 2026, when Rachel O'Brien stepped out of co-leading ([[08 Archive/Decisions/Decision History#DEC-069 — Women's Collective Co-Leader Change|DEC-069]], superseding DEC-044's intended Rachel–Miranda pairing). Each named leader remains subject to the applicable qualification, conversation, safeguarding, preparation, appointment, and readiness requirements.

## Gathering Shape

Collectives carry an approximately equal emphasis on fellowship and discipleship (DEC-053). They are accessible gatherings where people walk alongside other women of God, and they should become places where people find mentorship and lasting connection.

DEC-053 records devotional teaching followed by breakout-table discussion. Russ approved the flow ([[08 Archive/Decisions/Decision History#DEC-076 — Collective Gathering Flow, Six-Session Galatians, and Two-Page Handouts|DEC-076]]): 6:30–8:00 PM, 20 minutes check-in, two 30-minute blocks of teaching or devotional, then discussion, with teaching kept lighter than discussion (Russ named 15/15 or 10/20; [[08 Archive/Decisions/Decision History#DEC-085 — Lighter Teaching in Collective Study Blocks|DEC-085]]; breakout tables optional, depending on attendance), then regroup for 10 minutes of final thoughts and prayer requests and pray out. It follows the S.O.A.P. framework; see [[10 Templates/Gatherings/Collective Gathering#Gathering Flow (6:30–8:00 PM)|the canonical gathering template]]. Collective leaders and table leaders equip participants to take part. Table leaders follow the same leader standard and appointment path as other leaders (DEC-027).

## Galatians Study — Fall 2026–Spring 2027

Both Collectives work through all six chapters of Galatians in six sessions, one chapter per gathering ([[08 Archive/Decisions/Decision History#DEC-076 — Collective Gathering Flow, Six-Session Galatians, and Two-Page Handouts|DEC-076]], superseding DEC-067's twelve-gathering plan). Session 1 (Galatians 1) occurred October 6 for the men and October 13 for the women; Session 2 (Galatians 2) is October 20 and October 27. Thanksgiving, Christmas, New Year, and other holiday weeks will likely be skipped; exact breaks and later dates remain undecided.

Paul develops separate dated Men’s and Women’s course resources, including audience-specific formation goals, two-page participant handouts, teaching notes, and slides when needed. The common biblical research and chapter sequence stay connected. Russ, Miranda, and Conner operate the Collectives using Paul's resources. This clarifies DEC-055's earlier resource-ownership wording. Its shared Galatians study and connection to *The Fruit of the Spirit* stand; freedom remains the larger theme named there. Both Collectives stay on one study while Women's Collective carries its own focus in female discipleship. Rachel's earlier involvement in content discussions is not withdrawn by this clarification.

Paul uses Tim Keller’s *Galatians For You* as the primary course-development resource and source of discussion questions, and Eugene Peterson’s *Traveling Light* secondarily for its treatment of freedom, alongside the governing biblical text and his Notion knowledge sources. The two-page Session 2 (Galatians 2) handout for October 27 and the Session 1 (Galatians 1) redo are drafts awaiting Paul. These dated files are resource evidence, not a complete course calendar or gathering outcome (BWR-029).
