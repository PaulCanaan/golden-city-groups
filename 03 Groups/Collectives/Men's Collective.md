## Ministry Emphasis

Men's Collective is a confirmed expression of the Collectives branch, whose purpose is shared encouragement. Its gatherings should cultivate community, prayer, pastoral connection, biblical formation, and faithful response among men.

The approved long-term direction is for Men's Collective to mature into a broader Men's Ministry. This does not activate additional programs or establish their leaders, rhythms, or readiness.

The intended co-leads are Conner O'Brien and Russ Daly. Conner expressed interest during a Group Leader Conversation; that interest is not formal approval or commissioning. Each named leader remains subject to the applicable qualification, conversation, safeguarding, preparation, appointment, and readiness requirements.

## Gathering Shape

Collectives carry an approximately equal emphasis on fellowship and discipleship (DEC-053). They are accessible gatherings where people walk alongside other men of God, and they should become places where people find mentorship and lasting connection.

DEC-053 records devotional teaching followed by breakout-table discussion. Paul’s current working agenda is 6:30–8:00 PM: 20 minutes community/check-in, two blocks of 20 minutes teaching/exegesis + 10 minutes table discussion, then 10 minutes closing prayer/worship/announcements. This provisional flow will be adjusted after actual use and feedback; see [[10 Templates/Gatherings/Collective Gathering#Working 6:30–8:00 PM Flow|the canonical gathering template]]. ## Galatians Study — Fall 2026–Spring 2027

Both Collectives work through all six chapters of Galatians, with two study gatherings per chapter: twelve study gatherings per Collective, plus separate introductions ([[08 Archive/Decisions/Decision History#DEC-067 — Collective Resource Development and Galatians Course Plan|DEC-067]], planned by Russ and Paul and reported by Paul October 13). The every-other-week rhythm carries the course from fall into spring. Thanksgiving, Christmas, New Year, and other holiday weeks will likely be skipped; exact breaks and study dates remain undecided.

Paul develops separate dated Men’s and Women’s course resources, including audience-specific formation goals, 2–3-page participant handouts, table-leader notes, and slides when needed. The common biblical research and chapter sequence stay connected. Russ, Miranda, and Conner operate the Collectives using Paul's resources. This clarifies DEC-055's earlier resource-ownership wording. Its shared Galatians study and connection to *The Fruit of the Spirit* stand; freedom remains the larger theme named there. Both Collectives stay on one study while Men's Collective carries its own focus in male discipleship. Rachel's earlier involvement in content discussions is not withdrawn by this clarification.

Paul uses Tim Keller’s *Galatians For You* as the primary course-development resource and source of discussion questions, and Eugene Peterson’s *Traveling Light* secondarily for its treatment of freedom, alongside the governing biblical text and his Notion knowledge sources. Resource production is urgent for the coming study gatherings, with exact deadlines and finished files not yet recorded (BWR-029). The introductory gatherings introduce the Collectives within GCC Groups & Classes Ministry and help people get to know one another; they are outside the twelve study gatherings.

Paul reported the Galatians planning direction with Russ on October 13 (DEC-067). The wider construction meeting date and any remaining outcomes still need reconciliation under BWR-022; that report does not establish that the whole meeting occurred.
