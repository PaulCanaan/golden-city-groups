# Prayer Requests

## Document Status

**Awaiting Decision — operating guidance only.** PRO-026 and PRO-030 must establish approved confidentiality, consent, storage, sharing, retention, and escalation practices.

## Purpose

Help ministry environments pray with care while protecting people from unnecessary disclosure. This repository must not contain identifiable prayer requests.

## Minimum Draft Guidance

- Ask permission before sharing another person's request.
- Do not promise absolute confidentiality.
- Share only what is necessary with the approved audience and channel.
- Do not place prayer requests in this repository, general event notes, personal spreadsheets, or unapproved chat threads.
- Move suspected abuse, imminent danger, threats of harm, safeguarding concerns, or urgent pastoral needs to the approved escalation process immediately.
- Do not use prayer to pressure disclosure, offer diagnosis, or promise a particular outcome.

## Record Template

No prayer-request record is approved yet. When policy is established, this page may link to the authorized system and retention practice; it should not become the storage location.
