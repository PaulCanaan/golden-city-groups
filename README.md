# Golden City Groups

A ministry operations and formation repository for the Groups and Classes ministry of Golden City Church.

This repository contains the structures, systems, leader resources, and ministry documents used to build Christ-centered community, form disciples, and equip disciple-makers.

## Ministry Purpose

Golden City Groups exists to help people:

* **Belong** in Christ-centered community
* **Behold** Jesus through Scripture, worship, prayer, and shared life
* **Become** mature disciples who follow Jesus and help others do the same

Our aim is to create environments where people can forge faith, find freedom in Christ, and follow Jesus fully.

## Ministry Structure

The ministry is organized into two primary areas.

### Groups

Groups focus on community, discipleship, pastoral care, and shared spiritual formation.

Current and developing expressions may include:

* City Groups
* Home Groups
* Men’s and Women’s Collectives
* Sermon-based groups
* Seasonal discipleship groups
* Interest-based community gatherings

### Classes

Classes focus on biblical formation and equipping disciple-makers.

Current and developing expressions may include:

* Planted Bible Study
* Bible Club
* Biblical theology courses
* Leadership training
* Curriculum-based formation cohorts

## Formation Framework

The ministry is shaped by the framework:

> **Belonging → Beholding → Becoming**

* **Belonging:** People are welcomed into meaningful Christian community.
* **Beholding:** People encounter Jesus through Scripture, worship, prayer, and life together.
* **Becoming:** People are formed into faithful disciples and equipped to serve others.

## Contribution Guidelines

This repository may be developed by ministry leaders, volunteers, and approved collaborators.

When contributing:

1. Preserve the ministry’s mission and formation philosophy.
2. Avoid changing governance or safeguarding documents without review.
3. Use clear file names and consistent formatting.
4. Record significant structural decisions.
5. Keep operational documents practical and usable.
6. Protect confidential and personally identifiable information.

Do not upload private pastoral notes, sensitive member information, confidential counseling records, or protected personal data.
