## How to Use This Repository

This repository is intended for:

* Golden City Church ministry leaders
* Group leaders and coaches
* Class facilitators
* Ministry volunteers
* Pastors and church planters
* Churches developing groups and formation systems

Some documents are operational and specific to Golden City Church. Others may be adapted by churches building similar ministries.

Before using a resource publicly, please review it carefully and adapt it to your local church, pastoral context, safeguarding practices, and leadership structure.

## Current Development Priorities

Current areas of development include:

* City Group launch systems
* Group leader recruitment and training
* Ministry safeguarding and pastoral care
* Class and curriculum integration
* Attendance and feedback systems
* Seasonal ministry rhythms
* Leader coaching and evaluation
* Communication and invitation pathways

## Contribution Guidelines

This repository may be developed by ministry leaders, volunteers, and approved collaborators.

When contributing:

1. Preserve the ministry’s mission and formation philosophy.
2. Avoid changing governance or safeguarding documents without review.
3. Use clear file names and consistent formatting.
4. Record significant structural decisions.
5. Keep operational documents practical and usable.
6. Protect confidential and personally identifiable information.

Do not upload private pastoral notes, sensitive member information, confidential counseling records, or protected personal data.

## Public Use

This repository is public to support learning, collaboration, and resource sharing.

Public availability does not necessarily mean that every resource may be republished, sold, or represented as original work without permission. Please review the repository license and individual document notices before reuse.
