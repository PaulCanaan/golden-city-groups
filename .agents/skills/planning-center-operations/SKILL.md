# Planning Center Operations

Read [Planning Center Groups](../../../07%20Operations/Workflows/Planning%20Center%20Groups.md) and the relevant source. Use [Forms](../../../07%20Operations/Workflows/Forms.md) for intake, the [Leader Conversation Guide](../../../07%20Operations/Workflows/Leader%20Conversation%20Guide.md) for interview handoffs, [Group Launch Readiness](../../../07%20Operations/Workflows/Group%20Launch%20Readiness.md) for offering gates, or [Semester Planning](../../../07%20Operations/Workflows/Semester%20Planning.md) for rollover.

1. Identify the requested record type, current state, responsible person, and decision or evidence needed to advance. Verify the active vault and authorized Planning Center surface. If live access is unavailable, work from supplied evidence and label the result as a repository review.
2. Match the intended record and route before proposing changes. Separate leader interest, participant placement, and invited development. Check existing cards and permitted communication history before creating a card or preparing a repeat welcome. A returning leader with a completed application needs the seasonal review path or a human decision about re-entry.
3. Compare the evidence with the current stage in the operating procedure. An interview result comes from Paul. Training attendance, appointment, offering readiness, public listing, and actual first gathering are separate facts. A roster or scheduled start establishes neither actual launch nor a missing earlier stage. Record historic gaps as unknown.
4. Default to a read-only reconciliation. Give Paul the discrepancy, the source or observed state, the next action and owner, and any permission needed. Carry only aggregate status or non-sensitive operational follow-up into the vault. Keep personal and discernment records in the approved restricted system.
5. For an explicitly authorized live update, verify the current record and evidence before changing it, apply the approved change, and read back the result. Stop on an ambiguous match, an unapproved stage transition, or results that differ from the expected change. A test that submits a form, moves a card, or sends a message requires authorization for that action.

Russ retains participant-system ownership, pastoral appointment, and final offering readiness. Keep application invitations personal and manual. Use Paul’s approved next step for individual follow-up and the existing Biweekly Review Queue for unresolved cross-workflow issues.
